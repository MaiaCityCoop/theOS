## Idea

Samuel's words (typos tidied): "Any mini asset like a stone that you introduce, ensure it starts in the library as the
source of truth, right?" and "move reed and driftwood into its own intent in idea stage".

The river of intent 0004 has its shape (bends, cut banks, gravel bars) but its banks are bare: no reeds in the
shallows and wet edges, nothing washed up on the bars. Reeds and rushes (clumps in shallow water and along wet sand)
and driftwood (caught on the gravel bars) would make it lived-in. Each starts as a file in `assets/`, shown in the
library with its levels of detail, card and body, and is then scattered along the river by where it belongs.
