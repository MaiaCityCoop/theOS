# 0037 · What three videos teach the Bible

## Idea

> Let's create another session around fine tuning our storytelling bible... what can we learn from those 2 videos,
> that we upgrade our mission

Samuel pasted three transcripts (he called them two; the second channel has two):

1. **A solarpunk field researcher** on "a blueprint for utopia with ease": 50 communities visited, 800 rows of data;
   utopia as *vibes × solarpunk infrastructure*; two things that sink a project ("some guy's land", debt); energy and
   food sovereignty; five sources of vibes (solvency, distributed ownership, a strong door, aesthetics, sauce).
2. **"This is what AI should be used for"**: twelve real projects, each with its caveat. Its thesis: "The difference
   between writing your emails and saving a life was never the tool. It was the aim."
3. **"Elon Musk says saving money is pointless"**: the claim tested line by line. "What a machine can copy gets
   cheap. What takes a person's time or a particular place does not." "A promise can be kept. A law has to be."

What is wanted: read them against the Bible as it stands, keep what sharpens Maia City's mission, name what to reject,
and propose the upgrades as a before/after. **The mission itself (premise, logline, themes, the address) is Samuel's
call**: nothing in `Bible/story.md` changes before his yes. Research receipts and story ideas may go in now.

## Define

### Part 1 · What each video teaches, held against the Bible

Every point is marked **sharpens** (the Bible gets better), **confirms** (the Bible already says it; the video gives
a receipt or a better line), or **reject** (conflicts with a rule in Tone & Style, or with the premise).

### Part 2 · The proposed upgrades, before and after

Recommended, in plain words. **A–D are the mission: Samuel's call.** E–H follow from them and go in with the same yes.

**A · Premise**

- *Before:* "Living triumphs over surviving because people build a room where reaching is the norm and playing it safe
  is the exception, so nobody has to be brave to be their full size."
- *After:* "Living triumphs over surviving because people build **and own** a room where reaching is the norm and
  playing it safe is the exception, so nobody has to be brave to be their full size."
- *Why:* two words. Video 1's whole lesson is that a room on someone else's land is not safe to be large in. The
  counter-premise stays as it is.

**B · Logline, second sentence**

- *Before:* "…wants to build something larger than the life they have, and to own a piece of it."
- *After:* "…wants to build something larger than the life they have, and to own a piece of it: **the ground, the domes,
  and Maia herself.**"
- *Why:* the Bible says the only open question is who owns the machines and never answers it for the one machine
  in the story. This answers it.

**C · Themes**

- *Before:* living versus surviving · ownership as a human right · thinking big · abundance
- *After:* living versus surviving · ownership as a human right, **written down** · thinking big · abundance ·
  **the aim**
- *Why:* "a right written down, paid for before you need it" is what makes ownership a right and not a slogan.
  *The aim* ("it was never the tool, it was the aim") is the counter-image the Bible already draws in the push, with
  its name.

**D · The address, its last line**

- *Before:* "It is being built. A euro a day at a time, by people who stopped waiting to be told it was allowed."
- *After:* "It is being built. A euro a day at a time, **and owned by everyone who pays it**, by people who stopped
  waiting to be told it was allowed."
- *Why:* it still ends on a rung. It adds the one fact that makes the room safe, and asks nothing more.

**E · Tone & Style, two new rules**

1. **The better life either way.** The city is never sold as a shelter from something. No collapse, no "if it all
   falls apart". If a reason is needed, it is: it is the better way to live, whatever happens. (Video 1, 1.12.)
2. **Other builders are receipts, never rivals.** Twin Oaks, Te Hiku, Living Energy Farm appear only with a source, in
   their own words, credited; nobody is the opponent, named people least of all. (Videos 2 and 3.)

**F · New glossary terms**

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **The room and the roof** | The two halves of the city: the room (where reaching is normal) and the roof (domes, food, energy, ground). Many places have one; the city is both. |
| **The aim** | What the machine is pointed at. The same intelligence that keeps people comfortable elsewhere is pointed, here, at each person's full size, and the citizens hold the pointing. |
| **The charter** | Every right a citizen has, written down before it is needed and paid from money already collected. |
| **The gate and the door** | The gate (a euro, the game) is open to anyone. The door (a real dome, a village) is strong: it opens on what you built, never on who you are. |
