# 0006 · The water runs like a river's

## Idea

Samuel's words (typos tidied): "And now the water surface realism and currents etc.: what can we do there to get more
realistic?" Then: "Plan all of the optimisations that are not weather and sunlight related into an idea."

Today the water is one flat sheet: two ripple textures slide along the river's line and cross-fade; it runs faster in
the middle than at the edges but at the same speed all along; its colour comes from its depth, with a greyed sky as its
reflection, a sun glint, a thin foam line at the bank and a few floating specks.

What would make it run like a river's, without weather and without the sun's light (those come with intent 0007 and
the weather later):

1. **A flow field.** The current follows the bends; it runs fast where the river is narrow and deep and slow over the
   wide bars, fastest along a bend's outside (the cut bank), nearly still in a bar's lee, where a slow eddy turns.
   Ripples, foam and specks all travel with it, so you read where the water is quick.
2. **Riffles and foam.** Where shallow water runs fast (over a bar's edge, between boulders) the surface roughens:
   small standing waves, white water; foam streaks drift downstream along the current and gather in lines against
   the outer bank and in eddies.
3. **Seeing into it.** In the shallows the real bed shows through (the gravel, the sand, the stones), fading with the
   depth by how water takes up light: clear, then teal, then dark.
4. **Reflections of the world.** The banks, the grass on them and the tent mirrored in the water, not only a greyed
   sky: a cheap blurred mirror of the banks' colour first, true reflections only as far as the frame time allows.
5. **The water's edge.** A band of wet, darker sand where the water has just been; a small lapping at the edge; a
   soft, frayed waterline, not a clean cut.

Not here: wind ripples, rain rings, storm chop and fog over the water (the weather: the shading contract's slot), and
the sun's glitter and the light patterns on the bed (intent 0007, the sun and sky).
