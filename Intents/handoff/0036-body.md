## What is built (all on main)

- **Skeleton:** every bone, built against Gray's plates, in `TheGame/characters/kit/skeleton/`.
  - It has a rig of 90 joints with moves (`skeleton/pose.js`).
  - It is the library character Skeleton.
- **Muscles:** 206 muscles, defined as numbers only (origin, path, insertion, volume) in `characters/kit/muscles/table.js`.
  - They are drawn as strands and sheets by `muscles/build.js` and carried by the rig.
  - The shader that draws them is `muscles/flesh.js`.
  - They are the library character Muscles.
  - This round:
    - `BUILD` is 1.0, the studies' means.
    - A strand is lifted at most 8 mm over the muscles under it.
    - A sheet is thickened to at most 2× to reach its volume.
    - Many muscles were refitted against Samuel's two anatomical figures.
- **Fat and skin:** built by `characters/kit/skin/build.js` from the tables in `skin/table.js`. It is the library character **Body**, uid `f2d908f8-3c01-411d-a561-ef27148ba557`, with layers skin, fat, muscles and bones.
  - **How the skin is made:**
    - The muscles of each part are closed by their "fascia" (3.7 cm on the trunk and legs, 2.6 cm on the arms).
    - The tissue depth comes from `TISSUE`, a table of fat-thickness sites in mm.
    - The result is blended `OUTLINE = 0.5` with a drawn outline: `PROFILES` (sections every few cm) plus `MASSES` and `RELIEF`.
  - **Finer grids:** the head, hands and feet are built on 2 mm cells; the body uses 6 mm cells (8 mm on phones).
  - **Pieces and seams:** the head, hands and feet are separate mesh pieces, overlapping at their seams.
  - **Skinning:** each point is carried by its four nearest bones, with the weights smoothed over the trunk and thighs.
  - **Shader:** `muscles/flesh.js`, the skin material. Pores and grain, blush, veins, sheen, wrap diffuse, freckles.
  - **Face:** fitted to Richer's head. The lower face is set back by the `LOWER_FACE` warp; the head detail is mostly blurred away except at the front.
  - **Limbs (#50):**
    - a smooth shoulder top;
    - the latissimus taper;
    - a palm that runs past the knuckles, with the fingers webbed to it;
    - a carpus;
    - a slimmer wrist;
    - the ankle crossover;
    - calves.
- **Measured** on the skin (target in brackets): waist 82 (82) round and 29 across, hips 35 across, calf 39 (38), knee 37 (38), forearm 28, upper arm 33 (32).

## Gotchas

- **Worker build time:** the Body's muscles, fat and skin are built in one worker job.
  - About 15–50 s on desktop; a minute or more on a phone, and it may run out of memory.
  - The result is kept in IndexedDB, keyed by the tables' hash plus `MADE`.
  - **Bump `MADE`** in `skin/build.js` or `muscles/build.js` whenever their code changes, or browsers keep the old drawing.
- **Bones first:** since #51 the bones show until the skin is ready.
- **Layers:** `?layer=skin|fat|muscles|bones`. The bones grow only for the muscles and bones layers, or until the skin is ready.
- **Film camera:** the library frames through `cine.js`. Bars are suppressed by `cine.fill = true`, which the library sets.
- **Mid-line comments:** a comment placed in the middle of a line has swallowed code twice in this work. Always put comments at the end of a line or on their own line.
- **Stale renders:** the vite dev server dies after a while. Check `curl localhost:5173`, or your renders are stale.
- **Rest pose:** the arms hang straight down, so a flared trunk touches them and is shaded dark there.
- **No force-push** on shared branches. Branch from main for each round.
