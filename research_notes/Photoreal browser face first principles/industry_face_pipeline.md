## 1. Capture: what is captured for a hero head, what comes out, how it is conformed

### Takeaway
A hero head is a polarised light-stage or cross-polarised photogrammetry scan of a neutral plus a FACS-style expression set (tens of expressions), sometimes extended to 4D (a mesh per frame); outputs are sub-millimetre geometry and 4K-class diffuse/specular maps per expression, wrapped onto a fixed production topology. I found published numbers for the capture side but none for wrap-tool settings or a "standard" topology other than MetaHuman's.

### Cited Findings
- Digital Ira (Activision + USC ICT, 2013): actor scanned in **30 high-resolution expressions** on Light Stage X, giving **0.1 mm geometry and 4K diffuse and specular reflectance maps per expression**; **8 expressions** were chosen for the real-time rig. [X] — [Digital Ira poster](https://vgl.ict.usc.edu/Research/DigitalIra/digitalIraPoster.pdf), [project page](https://vgl.ict.usc.edu/Research/DigitalIra/)
- Light-stage outputs: normal, diffuse and specular reflectance maps from spherical gradient illumination (typically three gradients on X, Y, Z plus full-on); polarisation separates diffuse from specular; specular normals are embossed onto the base scan for high-resolution geometry (an early setup needed 13 photographs from two still cameras; a later one used 7 DSLRs and produced medium- and high-frequency displacement maps). [X]/[S] — [USC ICT face scanning](https://vgl.ict.usc.edu/Research/FaceScanning/), [Multiview capture](https://vgl.ict.usc.edu/Research/Multiview)
- Activision's own expanded ICT Light Stage uses **16 DSLRs** with polarised gradient illumination; game-team character artists then "regionalize mesh deltas to derive blendshapes for their specific face rigs", each team's rig having its own topology. [S] — [fxguide, Activision's virtual human](https://www.fxguide.com/?p=196609)
- Commercial photogrammetry example: Gentle Giant "Hydra" full-head/FACS array captures cross-polarised and specular textures with 50 MP Canon 5DS bodies and 20 flash units (camera count not stated). [X] — [Gentle Giant Studios](https://www.gentlegiantstudios.com/services/3d-scanning-services-los-angeles/vfx-head-and-facs-scanning/)
- inFAMOUS: Second Son (PS4, 2014): scanned face models were "approximately 1.4 mil polygons"; 75 people were scanned in three days for crowd faces. [S] — [Game Informer](https://www.gameinformer.com/games/infamous_second_son/b/playstation4/archive/2013/05/24/how-sucker-punch-photocopied-actors-for-infamous-second-son.aspx), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2013-05-26-sucker-punch-scans-random-faces-for-crowds-in-infamous-second-s.html)
- Ryse (Xbox One, 2013): pre-production sculpts reached 130 million polygons per character before reduction to the in-game mesh. [S] — [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/ryses-armor-compared-between-130-million-polygons-per-character-and-the-final-85000-on-xbox-one/)
- Unity "The Heretic" (2019–2020): first facial capture session at Infinite Realities; Infinite Realities and Russian3DScanner did the 4D processing; Snappers built the facial rig. 4D data is "a 3D scan per frame of animation", converted by external tools to meshes with frame-to-frame matching topology before import. [X] — [Unity, Making of The Heretic: Gawain](https://unity.com/blog/technology/making-of-the-heretic-digital-human-character-gawain), [Unity, tech package post](https://blog.unity.com/en/technology/making-of-the-heretic-digital-human-tech-package)
- Unity's package defines 4D as "a sequence of meshes captured over time" and ships "4D frame fitting allowing detail injection from facial rig". [P] — [Digital Human package README](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/com.unity.demoteam.digital-human)
- Conforming workflow as taught: clean stray geometry from the scan, wrap the base topology in Wrap3, refine in ZBrush; the neutral is the foundation for wrapping every expression (Metashape → Wrap → Maya/ZBrush). [S] — [Gnomon workshop, FACS pipeline](https://vt.thegnomonworkshop.com/blog/creating-facial-blendshapes-using-photogrammetry)
- Hellblade (2016–2017): 3Lateral used its own proprietary scanning system; Cubic Motion's tracker followed more than 200 facial features at over 90 fps to drive the rig live. [S] — [fxguide, Put your (digital) game face on](https://www.fxguide.com/?p=185219)

### Inferences
- The only widely published production topology with a vertex count is MetaHuman's head: 24,000 vertices at LOD 0 (section 8). Studio-specific topologies (Activision says each team has its own) are not published.
- Capture resolution (0.1 mm geometry, 4K maps per expression in 2013) is far above what ships: Digital Ira ran on a 4K-polygon bone-driven mesh (section 8). The scan's value at runtime is in the maps and the expression deltas, not the polygon count.
- Russian3DScanner is, to my knowledge, the vendor of the Wrap/Wrap4D tools; this was not confirmed from a source opened in this session.

### Gaps
- No source opened gave camera counts for a standard game-studio photogrammetry rig, nor Wrap3/Wrap4D settings, nor vertex counts for studio topologies other than MetaHuman.
- Displacement-map resolution delivered by a light-stage session: only the Graham et al. comparison (4K scanned mesostructure versus 16K synthesised microstructure, section 2) was found.
- MetaHuman Animator and Mesh to MetaHuman (Epic's own conform path) were not researched: Epic hosts blocked and search budget exhausted.

## 2. Texture stack: maps, resolutions, micro detail

### Takeaway
The shipped stack is albedo, normal, specular/roughness, a scatter (subsurface) mask, cavity/AO, plus tiling or synthesised micro-normal detail, with per-expression "animated" normal and colour deltas on top. Published hero resolutions are 4K (Epic's 2017–2018 digital humans) to 8K (MetaHuman Cine bake); animated deltas are much smaller (512 and 1K).

### Cited Findings
- Epic Digital Humans (Mike, 2017–2018; UE4): "all use 4K texture resolution produced from facial scans"; maps are "diffuse (albedo), roughness, specularity, scatter, and normal", with several normal maps blended for medium and micro detail. [X] — [UE 4.26 Digital Humans](https://docs.unrealengine.com/4.26/Resources/Showcases/DigitalHumans)
- MetaHuman: the highest-quality "Cine" assembly bakes normal and specular maps up to **8K**; the platform table lists maximum texture size **8192** for high-end PC and **2048** for iOS/Android (best LOD there is 3). [X] — [MetaHuman materials and textures](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-materials-and-textures), [MetaHuman platform and LOD specs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/metahuman/platform-support-and-lod-specifications-for-metahumans)
- MetaHuman animated maps are stored as pre-calculated **delta maps**, "512x512 by default for basecolor and 1K by default for normal", which Epic says allows much lower resolution to save memory. [X] — [MetaHuman materials and textures](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-materials-and-textures)
- MetaHuman baked assembly texture set: Base Color, a packed SRMF map (Specular, Roughness, Metallic, Fuzz), Opacity (used to modulate subsurface scattering), Normal; the unbaked face exposes base colour, normal, cavity and animated maps separately. [X] — [MetaHuman stylized texture editing](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-stylized-character-texture-and-material-editing), [Skin material tools](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/skin-material-tools)
- MetaHuman lower LODs: LOD 2 disables animated maps and multiplies AO into base colour "to help fake shadowing"; LOD 3 applies a baked normal map approximating LOD 0 normals. [X] — [MetaHuman materials and textures](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-materials-and-textures)
- Killzone Shadow Fall demo (PS4, 2013): six 2048×2048 textures on the body "plus detail maps and head textures"; authoring at 4K; Killzone 3 (PS3) used 1K textures. [X] — [Guerrilla postmortem PDF](https://www.guerrilla-games.com/media/News/Files/Valient_Killzone_Shadow_Fall_Demo_Postmortem.pdf)
- Ryse (Xbox One, 2013): texture density raised to an average 512 texels per metre from 256 in Crysis 3. [S] — [fxguide, The tech of Ryse](https://www.fxguide.com/?p=49486)
- Graham et al. 2013: skin patches digitised with polarised gradient illumination and macro photography at **10 µm** resolution; detail is synthesised onto a mesostructure-level scan; a figure compares a scanned **4K** displacement map with a synthesised **16K** one. [X] — [Measurement-Based Synthesis of Facial Microgeometry](https://vgl.ict.usc.edu/Research/Microgeometry/)
- Nagano et al. 2015: a 10 µm microstructure displacement map is **blurred along the stretch direction and sharpened along compression**, on the GPU "at interactive rates"; GLSL shader released for non-commercial research. [X] — [Skin Microstructure Deformation](https://vgl.ict.usc.edu/Research/SkinStretch)
- Digital Ira's shader blends diffuse, specular, normal and displacement by surface stress, with a "physically-based two-lobe specular reflection with microstructure". [X] — [Digital Ira poster](https://vgl.ict.usc.edu/Research/DigitalIra/digitalIraPoster.pdf), [Digital Ira and Beyond course](https://vgl.ict.usc.edu/Publications/2014/Digital%20Ira%20and%20Beyond%20Creating%20Photoreal%20Real-Time%20Digital%20Characters.pdf)
- Unity HDRP has a thickness map remapped to millimetres per diffusion profile for transmission, and a "Texturing Mode": Post-Scatter for scanned albedo "that already contain some blur due to subsurface scattering", Pre- and Post-Scatter otherwise. [P] — [HDRP diffusion profile reference](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/diffusion-profile-reference.md)
- The Heretic: 4D processing and de-noising smoothed the surface and lost pore-level detail, so the team added animated micro detail back. [X, from the Korean edition of the post] — [Unity, Making of The Heretic: Gawain](https://unity.com/kr/blog/technology/making-of-the-heretic-digital-human-character-gawain)

### Inferences
- Resolution is spent unevenly: static normal/specular at 4K–8K, expression deltas at 512–1K. A procedural head can copy that split: one high-frequency static layer plus low-resolution expression deltas.
- Scanned albedo already contains scattering blur, which is why engines offer a post-scatter mode; a procedural albedo has no such blur and wants the pre-and-post mode.
- Micro detail is added in two ways in the sources found: a tiling micro normal blended over the scan normal (Epic), or synthesised/measured displacement at 10 µm (ICT). Only the first is documented as shipped in an engine.

### Gaps
- Tile size and repeat count of micro-normal detail maps in MetaHuman or Call of Duty: not found.
- Texture resolutions for The Heretic and Enemies heads: not found (unity.com blocked; search budget ran out before the retry). One search summary attributed to a community README the claim that the skin shader "blends 82 different textures based on the face shape"; I could not locate or verify the page.
- Per-LOD texture sizes for MetaHuman (a 4K/2K tier table): the extract says none is given explicitly.

## 3. Expression: blendshapes versus joints, ARKit 52, MetaHuman, wrinkle and blood-flow maps

### Takeaway
Shipped rigs are hybrids: joints carry most deformation and scale down by LOD, corrective blendshapes run only at the top LOD, and expression-driven maps (wrinkle normals, colour/blood-flow deltas) are weighted by the same controls or by measured skin tension. MetaHuman's published LOD 0 is 713 joints plus 669 blendshapes; ARKit's 52 coefficients are the common capture-side interface.

### Cited Findings
- MetaHuman head, LOD 0–7: vertices 24,000 / 12,000 / 6,000 / 2,500 / 1,300 / 560 / 270 / 130; joints 713 / 529 / 397 / 283 / 84 / 70 / 41 / 26; **669 blendshapes at LOD 0 only**; animated maps at LOD 0 and 1 only. Epic calls these guidelines, not fixed values. [X] — [MetaHuman platform and LOD specs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/metahuman/platform-support-and-lod-specifications-for-metahumans)
- The skeleton is shared across LODs; lower LODs reduce joints by exclusion. [X] — [MetaHuman platform and LOD specs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/metahuman/platform-support-and-lod-specifications-for-metahumans)
- MetaHuman DNA file layers: Definition holds names of controls, joints, blend shapes, animated maps and meshes with their per-LOD mappings; Behavior maps GUI controls to raw controls and computes corrective expressions, joint transformations, blend shape channel weights and animated map weights; Geometry holds meshes, skin weights and blend shape deltas. [P] — [MetaHuman-DNA-Calibration docs/dna.md](https://github.com/EpicGames/MetaHuman-DNA-Calibration/blob/main/docs/dna.md)
- Rig Logic (3Lateral/Epic) is the runtime solver, built on FACS expressions; its stated innovations are pruning extraneous joint deformations, zero-cost reduction of joint configurations in LODs, and efficient storage and application of corrective expressions. [X] — [The tech behind MetaHuman Creator face rigs](https://www.metahuman.com/learning/the-tech-behind-metahuman-creator-face-rigs)
- MetaHuman expression parameters become weights for texture masks; normal maps WM1, WM2, WM3 and blood-flow colour maps CM1, CM2, CM3 are blended; the wrinkle mask array `T_head_wm_msk_Array` is about 40 MB. [S, forum posts, unofficial] — [UE forum tips thread](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/metahuman-tips-tricks/717924/3), [UE forum mask array thread](https://forums.unrealengine.com/t/metahuman-t-head-wm-msk-array-40-mb-vt-support-or-size-reduction-options/2719349)
- ARKit exposes a dictionary of blend shape coefficients keyed by `ARFaceAnchor.BlendShapeLocation` (jawOpen, eyeBlinkLeft, eyeSquintLeft …); third-party references list **52**, which one guide counts as 31 ignoring mirrored pairs. Apple describes them in its own terms; no FACS mapping is stated. [X]/[S] — [Apple BlendShapeLocation](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/arkit/arfaceanchor/blendshapelocation), [Ready Player Me ARKit list](https://docs.readyplayer.me/ready-player-me/api-reference/avatars/morph-targets/apple-arkit)
- Ryse (Xbox One, 2013): Marius had more than 770 joints, over 500 deforming, **260 facial joints**, **230 corrective facial blendshapes** running as "fixer" shapes on the joint rig, 8 skin influences; the facial rig was built with 3Lateral over four months and reused across characters. [S] — [fxguide, The tech of Ryse](https://www.fxguide.com/?p=49486), [Digital Production making-of](https://digitalproduction.com/2015/07/20/gladiatoren-in-der-pipeline/)
- Epic "Meet Mike" (2017): about **750 blendshapes** in the head mesh; the face rig has about 80 joints, mostly for hair and facial hair, with only about 10 on the face mesh (jaw, eyes, tongue). [S] — [fxguide](https://www.fxguide.com/?p=195972), [3DVF](https://3dvf.com/actualite-20903-siggraph-2017-meet-mike-un-humain-numerique-temps-reel-photorealiste-html/)
- Digital Ira (2013): mesh animation converted to standard bone animation on a **4K-polygon mesh** by a bone weight and transform solver. [X] — [Digital Ira poster](https://vgl.ict.usc.edu/Research/DigitalIra/digitalIraPoster.pdf)
- Uncharted 4 (PS4, 2016), press figures that conflict: 850 face poses versus 120 in Uncharted 3; faces with "between 300 and 500 bones"; Edge cites 800 animation bones in Drake's face. [S] — [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/uncharted-4-wip-screenshots-videos-info-show-the-incredible-tech-used-to-create-nathan-drake/), [Edge via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/australia/edge/20150115/282703340469865)
- Killzone Shadow Fall (PS4, 2013): up to 8 bone influences per vertex, most vertices 4–5, fewer at lower LODs. [X] — [Guerrilla postmortem PDF](https://www.guerrilla-games.com/media/News/Files/Valient_Killzone_Shadow_Fall_Demo_Postmortem.pdf)
- Oat 2007, "Animated Wrinkle Maps": **two wrinkle normal maps** (one squished, one stretched) plus the base normal map, wrinkle masks and animator-set weights. [X] — [Oat course notes](https://www.advances.realtimerendering.com/s2007/Oat%20-%20Wrinkles%20-%20Course%20notes%20(SIGGRAPH%202007).pdf)
- Jimenez, Echevarria, Oat, Gutierrez 2011 (GPU Pro 2): extends Oat to any number of wrinkle maps, each with a weight in [0, 1]. [S, as described by Raman et al.] — [Raman et al., WACV 2023](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2023/papers/Raman_Mesh-Tension_Driven_Expression-Based_Wrinkles_for_Synthetic_Faces_WACV_2023_paper.pdf), [Jimenez wrinkles paper](https://graphics.unizar.es/papers/JimenezWrinkles_low.pdf)
- Jimenez et al. 2010: skin colour modelled from in-vivo melanin and haemoglobin maps; base maps are built once and only haemoglobin changes are coded per expression; the authors state "negligible overhead" and coupling to a geometric rig. [X] — [A Practical Appearance Model for Dynamic Facial Color](https://www.iryoku.com/skincolor/)
- Unity Digital Human package: "Added skin tension to apply wrinkle maps"; the Snappers head renderer uses texture arrays. [P] — [Digital Human package README](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/com.unity.demoteam.digital-human)
- Enemies (2022): "tension tech for blood flow and wrinkle maps" removes the need for a facial rig for fine details. [S, community news roundup quoting Unity's feature list] — [RebusFarm news](https://static.rebusfarm.net/news/unity-enemies-real-time-cinematic-teaser), [Unity Enemies page](https://unity.com/demos/enemies)

### Inferences
- Two drivers exist for wrinkle and colour maps: rig control weights through region masks (Oat, Jimenez 2011, MetaHuman) and measured mesh tension (Unity Enemies, Raman et al.). Tension needs no rig and no hand-made masks, which suits a procedural head.
- Epic's own ladder shows what is expendable first: blendshapes go at LOD 1, animated maps at LOD 2, and joints fall by about 96% by LOD 7.
- Meet Mike (750 shapes, about 10 face joints) and Ryse (260 joints, 230 correctives) are opposite ends of the same hybrid; both are older hardware generations.

### Gaps
- Number of raw controls and corrective expressions in a MetaHuman rig, and Rig Logic timings: the whitepaper could not be opened ([English PDF link reported by a third party, unverified](https://cdn2.unrealengine.com/rig-logic-whitepaper-v2-5c9f23f7e210.pdf)).
- Number of wrinkle regions/masks and shader cost in Jimenez 2011 and Oat 2007: PDFs blocked; extracts gave no counts or timings.
- MetaHuman skin influences per LOD: one extract says 8 at LOD 0; my check was cut off by the search limit. Treat as unverified.
- Call of Duty's shipped rig figures (bones, shapes): no source found.

## 4. Skin shading in shipped real-time titles

### Takeaway
Three diffuse models dominate: pre-integrated (a lookup, no extra passes), separable screen-space (two passes, 11–25 taps per pass, roughly 0.4–0.5 ms at 1080p on 2013-era hardware) and Burley normalised diffusion sampled in screen space (Unreal up to 64 samples with temporal reuse; Unity 20/40/80 samples). All pair it with a dual-lobe specular and a thickness- or shadow-map-based transmission term.

### Cited Findings
- Separable SSS (Jimenez et al., CGF 2015): two convolutions "versus 12 in the sum-of-Gaussians approach"; with importance and stochastic sampling "only seven samples per pixel"; "under 0.5 ms per frame on an AMD Radeon HD 7970 at 1080p". The Activision-hosted expanded report gives **0.39 ms on PS4 at 1080p** for typical portrait shots. [X] — [CGF paper](https://graphics.unizar.es/papers/Separable-Subsurface-Scattering.pdf), [Expanded technical report](https://www.activision.com/cdn/research/Separable_Subsurface_Scattering_Expanded_Technical_Report_NEW.pdf)
- The public reference implementation ships three kernels: **11, 17 and 25 samples** (quality 0, 1, 2), "with respect to 1080p; for 720p Quality=0 suffices"; an optional follow-surface mode; transmittance from a shadow-map depth with scale `8.25 * (1 - translucency) / sssWidth`; SSS strength in the alpha channel. [P] — [iryoku/separable-sss SeparableSSS.h](https://github.com/iryoku/separable-sss/blob/master/SeparableSSS.h)
- Unreal's separable path stores kernels of 13, 9 and 6 non-mirrored samples (25, 17 and 11 total taps), the same three sizes; the profile fit is a sum of Gaussians (weights such as 0.113 and 0.358 visible) with the narrowest lobe treated as direct light. [PC] — [UE SubsurfaceProfileCommon.ush / SeparableSSS.cpp](https://github.com/EpicGames/UnrealEngine/blob/release/Engine/Shaders/Private/SubsurfaceProfileCommon.ush)
- Unreal Subsurface Profile defaults: scatter radius 1.2, IOR 1.55, **Roughness0 0.75, Roughness1 1.30, LobeMix 0.85**, Burley off by default in the struct; Burley defaults mean free path distance 1.2 × 2.229 and world unit scale 0.1. [PC], defaults confirmed by [X] — [UE SubsurfaceProfile.h](https://github.com/EpicGames/UnrealEngine/blob/release/Engine/Source/Runtime/Engine/Classes/Engine/SubsurfaceProfile.h), [UE Python API reference](https://docs.unrealengine.com/4.26/en-US/PythonAPI/class/SubsurfaceProfileStruct.html)
- Unreal Burley mode is a screen-space model enabled per profile and "requires Temporal Anti-Aliasing to display properly"; transmission tint works only with Burley. [X] — [UE Subsurface Profile docs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/subsurface-profile-shading-model-in-unreal-engine)
- Unreal Burley shader: maximum **64 samples**, adaptive counts 8 (high luminance), 16 (low luminance), 32 (profile edge), bilateral filtering and temporal reprojection on; a half-resolution mode falls back to the separable filter. [PC] — [UE SubsurfaceBurleyNormalized.ush](https://github.com/EpicGames/UnrealEngine/blob/release/Engine/Shaders/Private/SubsurfaceBurleyNormalized.ush)
- Unreal lighting for profile materials: older method splits diffuse and specular in a checkerboard; newer method reconstructs full-resolution lighting in the subsurface pass, "typically faster than checkerboard" with a 64-bit scene colour format. [X] — [UE Subsurface Profile docs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/subsurface-profile-shading-model-in-unreal-engine)
- Unreal dual specular: two GGX lobes mixed by LobeMix with one shared visibility term from the average roughness; source comment: "Disable dual lobe, as subsurface profile doesn't work with forward." A separate `PreintegratedSkinBxDF` shading model exists. [PC] — [UE ShadingModels.ush](https://github.com/EpicGames/UnrealEngine/blob/release/Engine/Shaders/Private/ShadingModels.ush)
- MetaHuman scalability: the Medium quality setting disables subsurface scattering. [X] — [MetaHuman materials and textures](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-materials-and-textures)
- Unity HDRP: SSS is "a screen-space blur technique" (Burley normalised diffusion, per the linked SIGGRAPH 2018 talk); default sample budgets **Low 20, Medium 40, High 80, Max 1000**; up to 15 custom diffusion profiles in view; skin IOR default 1.4; the High Quality option is "around two and a half times more resource intensive than the default quality"; SSS is not applied when the profile's max radius is smaller than a pixel on screen. [P] — [HDRP SSS docs](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/skin-and-diffusive-surfaces-subsurface-scattering.md), [HDRP diffusion profile reference](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/diffusion-profile-reference.md), [DiffusionProfileSettings.cs](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Runtime/Material/DiffusionProfile/DiffusionProfileSettings.cs)
- Unity HDRP dual lobe: two smoothness multipliers and a Lobe Mix (default 0.5) per profile, "to simulate the thin oily layer"; for reflection probes and SSR only a single lobe is evaluated "for performance reasons"; Diffuse Shading Power example raised from 1 to 1.9; transmission modes Thick (thickness from shadow map, max with baked thickness) and Thin. [P] — [HDRP diffusion profile reference](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/diffusion-profile-reference.md)
- Burley's profile as implemented: R(r) = A·s/(8πr)·(e^(−sr) + e^(−sr/3)), with an analytically invertible CDF for importance sampling. [PC, blog copy in scratchpad] — [Golubev, Sampling Burley's Normalized Diffusion Profiles](https://zero-radiance.github.io/post/sampling-diffusion/)
- Pre-integrated skin (Penner, SIGGRAPH 2011 / GPU Pro 2): a simple pixel shader, all inputs in textures and bump maps, **no blur passes**; the lookup is indexed by N·L and built by ring integration of the sum-of-Gaussians profile. [X]/[S] — [Penner slides](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/penner-preintegrated-skin-rendering-siggraph-2011-advances-in-realtime-rendering-course/13966747), [Skin Rendering notes](https://progmdong.github.io/2019-02-03/Skin_Rendering)
- d'Eon and Luebke (GPU Gems 3 ch. 14, 2007): texture-space diffusion with separable, hierarchical convolutions of a sum-of-Gaussians profile and a stretch-correction texture; a secondary source says six Gaussians fit the three-layer skin profile. [X]/[S] — [GPU Gems 3 chapter 14](https://developer.nvidia.com/gpugems/GPUGems3/gpugems3_ch14.html), [UIC course page](https://www.evl.uic.edu/sjames/cs525/project3.html)
- Digital Ira's DirectX 11 renderer: screen-space SSS, translucency, eye refraction and caustics, real-time ambient shadows, two-lobe specular with microstructure, depth of field, antialiasing, film grain. [X] — [Digital Ira poster](https://vgl.ict.usc.edu/Research/DigitalIra/digitalIraPoster.pdf)
- Frostbite 2 / Battlefield 3 (2011): a cheap translucency approximation designed to avoid extra depth maps and texture-space blurs. [X] — [Frostbite, Approximating Translucency](https://www.frostbite.com/frostbite/news/approximating-translucency-for-a-fast-cheap-and-convincing-subsurface-scattering-look)

### Inferences
- Cost ladder, cheapest first: pre-integrated (no pass) < separable at 11 taps × 2 passes < separable at 25 taps × 2 < Burley at 20–80 stochastic samples needing temporal accumulation. The 0.39–0.5 ms figures are for 2013 desktop/PS4 GPUs at 1080p; no browser or mobile figure was found.
- Both Burley implementations lean on temporal anti-aliasing to hide noise. A renderer without TAA should prefer the separable kernel or pre-integration.
- Dual-lobe specular costs one extra GGX evaluation; Unreal shares the visibility term and Unity drops the second lobe for probes. Unreal's comment that the profile "doesn't work with forward" concerns its G-buffer profile lookup, not the maths.

### Gaps
- Which skin model each Call of Duty title ships: not found beyond Activision hosting the separable SSS report.
- Current Frostbite skin model (Battlefront, Battlefield, FC): not found.
- Pre-integrated skin cost in ms and its use in named shipped titles: not found.
- Unreal Substrate-era skin and any 2025–2026 changes: search budget ran out.

## 5. Eyes, teeth, hair

### Takeaway
The standard eye is a two-layer model (wet cornea over iris and sclera) with refraction, limbal darkening, iris caustics, scleral scattering, and extra blend geometry or passes at the lid line; Unity tiers it by cost. Scalp hair is strands at the top LOD (50,000 in MetaHuman) and cards below; in Epic's Mike, hair took 75% of 440,000 triangles.

### Cited Findings
- Unity HDRP eye: a two-layer material, cornea and surface fluid over sclera and iris, supporting "cornea refraction, caustics, pupil dilation, limbal darkening, and subsurface scattering". [P] — [HDRP Understand eyes](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/understand-eyes.md)
- HDRP eye tiers: **Eye** (low-cost caustics), **Eye Cinematic** (refracts incoming light; "the algorithm used in The Heretic"), **Eye Cinematic With Caustic** ("used in Enemies … the most resource intensive"). The sample eye graph depends on the sample mesh's vertex positions and does not work with custom meshes. [P] — [HDRP eye material type](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/snippets/shader-properties/surface-options/eye-material-type.md), [Create an eye material](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/create-an-eye-material.md)
- HDRP eye inputs: iris normal, iris plane offset, iris radius, IOR, mask, diffusion profile, caustic intensity and blend. [P] — [HDRP Eye Master Stack reference](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/eye-master-stack-reference.md)
- Unity Digital Human package: "custom pass for cross-material normal buffer blur (tearline)", "custom marker-driven occlusion for eyes and teeth", "custom pass for slight blurring around the eye lids". [P] — [Digital Human package README](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/com.unity.demoteam.digital-human)
- Unity package code comment: the real corneal index of refraction is 1.376 "but our geometry works well with a value of 1.2"; limbal darkening is a Z offset from the cornea. [X, from a repository mirror] — [Digital Human commit mirror](https://plastichub.unity.cn/unity-tech-cn/com-unity-demoteam-digital-human/commit/cda478a6-7734-4346-a343-9915af68ee54)
- Unreal Eye shading model: G-buffer carries an iris normal, an iris mask and an iris distance; F0 = Specular × 0.08; iris diffuse is multiplied by a caustic term `0.8 + 0.2·(P+1)·pow(dot(CausticNormal, L), P)` with P = lerp(12, 1, IrisNoL); the caustic normal blends the iris normal toward −N to fake concavity. [PC] — [UE ShadingModels.ush](https://github.com/EpicGames/UnrealEngine/blob/release/Engine/Shaders/Private/ShadingModels.ush)
- Epic's advice: take the eye geometry and material from the Digital Humans sample and replace textures instead of building an eye material from scratch. [X] — [UE Digital Humans docs](https://docs.unrealengine.com/4.26/Resources/Showcases/DigitalHumans)
- HDRP border attenuation darkens SSS near material borders and is "especially beneficial for rendering complex surfaces like eyes". [P] — [HDRP SSS docs](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/skin-and-diffusive-surfaces-subsurface-scattering.md)
- Teeth: a 2020 CGF paper states realistic teeth cannot be done with opaque diffuse textures alone, mainly because of subsurface scattering; Reallusion's teeth/gum shader has separate scatter for gums and teeth and an AO mask "to conceal over-lit inner teeth and tongue"; an artist write-up uses a transition mesh between teeth and gums so screen-space SSS does not leave a hard edge. [X]/[S] — [Reischl et al., CGF](https://diglib.eg.org/handle/10.1111/cgf13665), [Reallusion Digital Human Shader](https://reallusion.com/character-creator/digital-human-shader.html), [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/001agt-002mrs-organic-art-realistic-subdermal-shaders)
- MetaHuman hair by LOD: hair-style **strands 50,000 / 25,000** (LOD 0–1 only); hair-style **card vertices 30,000 / 15,000 / 10,000 / 3,000 / 1,500** (LOD 0–4); hair-style mesh vertices 500 / 250 / 100 (LOD 5–7); **facial hair strands 10,000 / 5,000**; facial hair card vertices 15,000 / 7,000 / 3,000 / 1,000 / 500. By default strands at LOD 0, cards below. [X] — [MetaHuman platform and LOD specs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/metahuman/platform-support-and-lod-specifications-for-metahumans), [Controlling MetaHuman LODs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/controlling-metahuman-levels-of-detail-lods-in-unreal-engine)
- Meet Mike (2017): about 440,000 triangles, roughly 75% for hair. [S] — [fxguide](https://www.fxguide.com/?p=195972), [80.lv](https://80.lv/articles/new-standards-of-real-time-digital-rendering-from-siggraph)
- Frostbite strand hair: about 10,000 strands simulated and rendered for the test asset; shown in FIFA 21. [X]/[S] — [Frostbite hair rendering and simulation, part 2](https://www.ea.com/able/news/frostbite-hair-rendering-and-simulation-2), [GfxSpeak](https://gfxspeak.com/?p=64741)
- Research baseline for cards: a hair-card model with three LODs of 600K, 300K and 70K triangles. [X] — [Real-time LOD strand-based hair rendering](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2405.10565)
- Sorting and antialiasing: HDRP says semi-transparent cards must be sorted manually; its High Quality Line Renderer is a software rasteriser with 8×8-pixel clusters, front-to-back sorted segments, analytic coverage and an opacity early-out. Software rasterisation with tiled depth-bucket OIT (Taillandier and Valdes 2020) is described as standard in high-end engines. A practitioner guide says shipped games use masked alpha with a tuned clip value. [P]/[X]/[S] — [HDRP high quality line rendering](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/understand-high-quality-line-rendering.md), [Implicit strand hair via software rasterization](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.04230), [StraySpark guide](https://www.strayspark.studio/blog/blender-hair-grooming-to-unreal-engine-pipeline-2026)
- HDRP hair shading: Approximate (Kajiya-Kay, cards, cheaper, best for dark hair) versus Physical (Marschner, cards and strands, with approximate multiple scattering for light hair). [P] — [HDRP Hair Master Stack reference](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/hair-master-stack-reference.md)
- Unity's skin attachment system drives eyebrows, eyelashes and stubble from the deforming skin; Enemies added a GPU path for dense meshes such as peach fuzz; Unity's strand solver handles "tens of thousands of individual strands". [P]/[X] — [Digital Human package README](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/com.unity.demoteam.digital-human), [Unity hair package README](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/com.unity.demoteam.hair), [Unity Enemies page](https://unity.com/demos/enemies)

### Inferences
- Lid-line treatment is done three ways in the sources: a normal-buffer blur across materials (Unity tearline), marker-driven analytic occlusion (Unity), and blend/occlusion meshes (Epic's sample, not confirmed here). The first two need no extra geometry.
- Unreal's caustic is a few instructions on top of Lambert; it is the cheapest published iris caustic and needs only an iris normal and mask.
- Hair is the largest geometry cost of a head at every tier; eyebrows and lashes sit in the 5,000–10,000 strand band in MetaHuman.

### Gaps
- Epic's documentation of the eye occlusion, tear-line and lacrimal meshes: not retrieved (hosts blocked; the search returned third-party material only).
- Strand or card counts for eyelashes, eyebrows and peach fuzz as separate rows: not found; MetaHuman lists only "facial hair".
- MetaHuman and Call of Duty teeth/gum shading: nothing found.
- Triangle counts of hair in a named shipped console title: not found.

## 6. Lighting and presentation for digital-human showcases

### Takeaway
Showcases light a head like a portrait: an HDRI sky for fill plus a few large area key lights, soft and contact shadows, temporal antialiasing, depth of field and a filmic tone curve. I found feature lists from Epic, Unity and Activision, but no opened source in which a studio ranks which of these is essential.

### Cited Findings
- Epic and cinematographer Greig Fraser released MetaHuman lighting presets (Portrait, Split, Fireside, Moonlight, Tungsten, Red Lantern) matching those inside MetaHuman Creator; Portrait is modelled on studio beauty lighting. [X] — [British Cinematographer](https://britishcinematographer.co.uk/?p=50558), [Epic lighting presets post](https://www.unrealengine.com/zh-CN/blog/six-new-character-lighting-presets-for-unreal-engine-available-now)
- Epic's custom MetaHuman lighting scenario: a skydome with an HDRI map paired with a SkyLight, plus lights parented to a rotator. [X] — [MetaHuman custom lighting scenarios](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/working-with-additional-actors-for-custom-metahuman-lighting-scenarios-in-unreal-engine)
- Enemies (2022) ran in HDRP at 4K with screen-space global illumination, adaptive probe volumes, ray tracing and DLSS. [X] — [Unity, Introducing Enemies](https://unity.com/blog/news/introducing-enemies-the-latest-evolution-in-high-fidelity-digital-humans-from-unity)
- Digital Ira's presentation layer: real-time ambient shadows, depth of field, antialiasing, film grain. [X] — [Digital Ira poster](https://vgl.ict.usc.edu/Research/DigitalIra/digitalIraPoster.pdf)
- HDRP contact shadows are ray-marched in the depth buffer at close range for detail shadow maps miss; 24 lights can cast them at once. Micro shadows estimate shadowing of normal-map detail from the normal and AO maps, for directional lights only. [P] — [HDRP contact shadows](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/Override-Contact-Shadows.md), [HDRP micro shadows](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/Override-Micro-Shadows.md)
- HDRP tone mapping modes are None, Neutral, ACES, Custom and External; ACES is "more contrasted than Neutral and has an effect on actual color hue & saturation". AgX is not listed. [P] — [HDRP tonemapping](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/Post-Processing-Tonemapping.md)
- A Reallusion render guide recommends ACES or AgX for cinematic work. [S] — [Reallusion forum guide](https://discussions.reallusion.com/t/rtx-render-open-beta-2-render-settings-lights-camera-sky/17903)
- Unreal's Burley skin needs temporal antialiasing; Unity's SSS noise is "blurred by TAA" (source comment). [X]/[PC] — [UE Subsurface Profile docs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/subsurface-profile-shading-model-in-unreal-engine), [HDRP SubsurfaceScattering.compute](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Runtime/Material/SubsurfaceScattering/SubsurfaceScattering.compute)

### Inferences
- Temporal antialiasing is load-bearing in both engines: it hides SSS sampling noise as well as hair and specular aliasing.
- Dual-lobe specular is only evaluated for analytic lights in HDRP, so key lights rather than the HDRI carry the skin's sheen; this matches the HDRI-fill plus key-light setups.

### Gaps
- PCSS or ray-traced shadow settings used in MetaHuman showcases, and Epic's render-settings page for MetaHumans: not retrieved.
- A studio statement ranking lighting features: not found.
- Use of AgX in any engine showcase: not found.

## 7. Contribution to realism per component, and what is cheapest to adopt

### Takeaway
Studios rarely rank components in the sources I could reach; the best evidence is what they switch off first when scaling down and what they call cheap. By that measure dynamic colour maps, dual-lobe specular, pre-integrated or 11-tap separable SSS and a simple iris caustic are the cheap adoptions; strand hair, corrective blendshapes and cinematic eyes are the expensive ones.

### Cited Findings
- Epic's scale-down order for MetaHuman: blendshapes exist only at LOD 0; strands only at LOD 0–1; animated maps off from LOD 2 with AO baked into base colour; baked normals from LOD 3; Medium quality drops SSS. [X] — [MetaHuman platform and LOD specs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/metahuman/platform-support-and-lod-specifications-for-metahumans), [MetaHuman materials and textures](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-materials-and-textures)
- Unity says SSS makes skin "look smooth and natural rather than rough and plastic-like". [P] — [HDRP SSS docs](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/skin-and-diffusive-surfaces-subsurface-scattering.md)
- Unity found that losing pore-level detail in 4D processing had to be compensated with animated micro detail. [X] — [Unity, Making of The Heretic: Gawain](https://unity.com/kr/blog/technology/making-of-the-heretic-digital-human-character-gawain)
- Jimenez et al. describe dynamic facial colour as adding "negligible overhead". [X] — [Dynamic facial color](https://www.iryoku.com/skincolor/)
- Separable SSS costs 0.39 ms on PS4 at 1080p in portrait shots; pre-integrated skin needs no blur pass. [X] — [Expanded technical report](https://www.activision.com/cdn/research/Separable_Subsurface_Scattering_Expanded_Technical_Report_NEW.pdf), [Penner slides](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/penner-preintegrated-skin-rendering-siggraph-2011-advances-in-realtime-rendering-course/13966747)
- Unity grades its three eye models by cost, with the caustic version "the most resource intensive". [P] — [HDRP eye material type](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Documentation~/snippets/shader-properties/surface-options/eye-material-type.md)
- In Meet Mike three quarters of the triangles went to hair. [S] — [fxguide](https://www.fxguide.com/?p=195972)

### Inferences
- Cheapest to adopt, in my reading of the costs above: (1) dual-lobe specular, one extra GGX term; (2) haemoglobin/colour delta per expression at 512²; (3) wrinkle normal deltas at 1K blended by tension or masks; (4) pre-integrated SSS, or separable SSS at 11 taps for 720p-class output; (5) Unreal-style iris caustic plus limbal darkening; (6) lid-line occlusion without extra meshes.
- Most expensive: strand hair (50,000 strands), 669 corrective blendshapes, 64-sample Burley SSS with temporal reuse, refractive cinematic eyes.
- Epic keeps SSS longer than animated maps on the LOD ladder but drops it at Medium quality; the two scales are separate and should not be merged into one ranking.

### Gaps
- No opened source has a studio saying "component X contributes most". Talks likely to contain this (Jimenez's GDC 2013 "Next-Generation Character Rendering", Hable's GDC 2014 "From Facial Scans to Facial Animation", the SIGGRAPH 2014 Digital Ira course) are on blocked hosts.
- No per-component millisecond breakdown for a full head (skin + eyes + hair) was found for any engine.

## 8. Published budgets

### Takeaway
Four budgets could be documented with sourced numbers: MetaHuman (current), Epic's Meet Mike (2017), Activision/ICT Digital Ira (2013) and two console launch titles, Killzone Shadow Fall and Ryse (2013). Unity's Heretic/Enemies package documents features and tiers but, in what I could open, almost no counts.

### Cited Findings
- **MetaHuman (Epic documentation as indexed by search in November 2026; the pages mention the "Cine" assembly tier, so they postdate MetaHuman's move into the engine).** Head LOD 0–7 vertices 24,000 / 12,000 / 6,000 / 2,500 / 1,300 / 560 / 270 / 130; joints 713 / 529 / 397 / 283 / 84 / 70 / 41 / 26; 669 blendshapes (LOD 0); animated maps LOD 0–1; hair strands 50,000 / 25,000; hair card vertices 30,000 / 15,000 / 10,000 / 3,000 / 1,500; facial hair strands 10,000 / 5,000; textures to 8192 on high-end PC, 2048 on mobile; animated deltas 512² colour, 1K normal. Body LOD 0 is 30,500 vertices in the Japanese and Chinese editions of the page. [X] — [MetaHuman platform and LOD specs](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/metahuman/platform-support-and-lod-specifications-for-metahumans), [MetaHuman materials and textures](https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/metahuman/metahuman-materials-and-textures)
- **Epic Meet Mike (2017, PC VR).** About 440,000 triangles, 75% hair; about 750 blendshapes; about 80 rig joints, about 10 on the face; 4K scan-derived textures; rendered at 90 fps in VR, about 9 ms per stereo frame; the live show used nine synced high-end PCs. [S] — [fxguide](https://www.fxguide.com/?p=195972), [Road to VR](https://www.roadtovr.com/?p=66675), [UE Digital Humans docs](https://docs.unrealengine.com/4.26/Resources/Showcases/DigitalHumans)
- **Activision / USC ICT Digital Ira (2013).** 30 expressions scanned at 0.1 mm with 4K diffuse and specular maps each; 8 used at runtime; bone animation on a 4K-polygon mesh; DirectX 11; target and result **180 fps** full-screen on a GeForce GTX 680 (one source says at 720p). [X]/[S] — [Digital Ira poster](https://vgl.ict.usc.edu/Research/DigitalIra/digitalIraPoster.pdf), [Jimenez blog](https://www.iryoku.com/?p=1639), [fxguide, Digital Emily to Digital Ira](https://www.fxguide.com/fxfeatured/the-art-of-digital-faces-at-ict-from-digital-emily-to-digital-ira/)
- **Call of Duty / Activision skin pass.** Separable SSS at 0.39 ms on PS4, 1080p, portrait shots. [X] — [Expanded technical report](https://www.activision.com/cdn/research/Separable_Subsurface_Scattering_Expanded_Technical_Report_NEW.pdf)
- **Killzone Shadow Fall demo postmortem (PS4, 2013).** Characters about 40,000 polygons at the top LOD, seven LODs down to 150 polygons beyond 30 m; up to 8 bone influences; six 2048² body textures plus detail maps and head textures; target 1080p at 30 fps. Killzone 3: 10,000 polygons, three LODs, 1K textures. [X] — [Guerrilla postmortem PDF](https://www.guerrilla-games.com/media/News/Files/Valient_Killzone_Shadow_Fall_Demo_Postmortem.pdf), [SlideShare copy](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/killzone-shadow-fall-demo-postmortem/21167452)
- **Ryse: Son of Rome (Xbox One, 2013).** Hero 85,000 polygons (in-game characters about 40K at first LOD); 770+ joints, 260 facial; 230 corrective facial blendshapes; 8 skin influences; 512 texels per metre; rendered at 1600×900. [S] — [fxguide, The tech of Ryse](https://www.fxguide.com/?p=49486), [DualShockers](https://www.dualshockers.com/ryses-armor-compared-between-130-million-polygons-per-character-and-the-final-85000-on-xbox-one/)
- **Unity Heretic / Enemies.** Package requirements Unity 2020.3 and HDRP 10.9 minimum; GPU skin deformation needs 2021.2; new eye and skin graphs need 2022.2 and HDRP 14. Features: 4D clip import and fitting, Snappers rig, skin attachment for brows, lashes and stubble, skin/eye/teeth/hair shader graphs, tearline normal blur, marker-driven eye and teeth occlusion, skin tension for wrinkle maps. HDRP budgets that apply: SSS 20/40/80 samples, 15 diffusion profiles, three eye tiers. [P] — [Digital Human package README](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/com.unity.demoteam.digital-human), [DiffusionProfileSettings.cs](https://github.com/Unity-Technologies/Graphics/blob/master/Packages/com.unity.render-pipelines.high-definition/Runtime/Material/DiffusionProfile/DiffusionProfileSettings.cs)

### Inferences
- A decade of budgets brackets the head mesh narrowly: 4K polygons (Digital Ira, 2013), 24,000 vertices (MetaHuman LOD 0), and 6,000 or 2,500 vertices at MetaHuman LOD 2–3 where Epic targets mobile. Photoreal claims rest on maps and shading more than on mesh density.
- Digital Ira's 180 fps on a 2012 GPU with the full feature list suggests the shading stack itself is light; MetaHuman's weight is in joints, shapes and strands.
