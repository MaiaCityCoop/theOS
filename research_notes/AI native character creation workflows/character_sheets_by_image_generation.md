## 1. Which image models lead for identity-consistent, photoreal people, and what are their current versions

### Takeaway
As of mid-November 2026 the models that take several reference images and are positioned for identity-preserving edits are OpenAI GPT Image 2 / 2.5 / 2.6 (up to 16 reference images), Google Nano Banana Pro / 2 / 2.1 (up to 14), Black Forest Labs FLUX.2 and FLUX 3 Image (8 to 10), ByteDance Seedream 5.0 / 5.5 (10 to 14) and Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 (open weights, Apache 2.0); Midjourney V8.2 folds character reference into an "Edit Model" with four references and has no API. I found no public benchmark that measures identity across the views of a turnaround (front, profile, back); the published evidence says that general multi-reference editors still drift on strict identity, so the sheet has to be checked view by view.

### Inferences
- The field has moved from identity adapters bolted onto a diffusion model (IP-Adapter, InstantID, PuLID, all 2023 to 2024) to general editing models that take many reference images in context. For a new pipeline the adapters are the fallback (local, open, controllable), not the first choice.
- The count of reference slots matters for a turnaround: with 10 to 16 slots, each new view can be conditioned on the master portrait plus every view already approved, which is the main lever against drift.
- For a photoreal human whose face must survive front, profile and back views, a LoRA trained on the approved sheet is the only technique any source calls "very high" consistency; it is worth a second pass once a first sheet exists.
- Rankings change monthly (GPT Image 2 in July, MAI-Image-2.5-Pro in August, GPT Image 2.5 Sunburst later). Choosing by a small in-house test on the project's own character is more reliable than choosing by leaderboard.

### Gaps
- No benchmark found that measures face identity across turnaround views (profile and back) for the November 2026 models. Whether one exists could not be checked, because arXiv and the leaderboards could not be opened.
- What GPT Image 2.6, Nano Banana 2.1 and Seedream 5.5 change for identity is unknown: only their ids, limits and prices were read.
- Midjourney V8.2, Firefly Image 5 and Imagen 4: no primary source read; how Firefly Image 5 takes an identity reference was not found at all.
- SeedEdit: no current model id found; it may have been merged into Seedream's edit mode, but no source says so.

## 2. Proven techniques and prompt patterns for turnaround sheets; dedicated multi-view models; do image-to-3D tools make the other views themselves

### Takeaway
The pattern the sources agree on is: lock one approved master image first, then produce each view as a separate edit conditioned on the master (and on views already approved), with an immutable text "anchor" repeated in every prompt and an explicit list of what must not change; a single combined sheet is good for a first look but is the form in which proportions and angles go wrong. Dedicated multi-view diffusion models (Era3D, MV-Adapter, CharacterGen, PSHuman) all date from before mid-2025 and work at 512 to 768 pixels; their place has been taken by camera-control LoRAs on Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 and FLUX.2, and by the 3D generators' own view synthesis (Tripo has an "Image to Multiview" step).

### Cited Findings

**Prompt patterns**
- fal's character-design skill separates an immutable anchor from a per-shot variable block: `CHARACTER ANCHOR: [codename], [age range], [face shape], [eye shape and color], [brow shape], [nose], [mouth], [skin details], [hair color, length, texture, and style], [build and posture], [signature wardrobe or accessory], [visual style]` and `SHOT VARIABLE: [expression], [pose/action], [outfit allowed to change or not], [setting], [camera distance and angle], [lighting], [mood], [output format]`. "Never rewrite the anchor casually. If a result changes identity, strengthen the anchor or switch to a reference/edit workflow instead of adding more style words." **[READ]** — [fal-ai-community/skills, character-design](https://github.com/fal-ai-community/skills/blob/main/skills/character-design/SKILL.md); [anchor-system.md](https://github.com/fal-ai-community/skills/blob/main/skills/character-design/references/anchor-system.md)
- Its turnaround pattern: "character turnaround sheet with front view, side view, back view, consistent proportions and outfit, neutral standing pose, clean white background, production model sheet layout, no style drift between views". Its full-body pattern adds "entire silhouette visible … no cropped feet". **[READ]** — [fal-ai-community/skills, prompt-patterns.md](https://github.com/fal-ai-community/skills/blob/main/skills/character-design/references/prompt-patterns.md)
- Its escalation ladder: "1. Text-only anchor for exploration. 2. Approved image reference for continuity. 3. Edit or reference-image workflow for outfit and expression variations." **[READ]** — [fal-ai-community/skills, fal-recipes character-design](https://github.com/fal-ai-community/skills/blob/main/skills/fal-recipes/references/character-design.md)
- OpenAI's guide: "For edits, use 'change only X' + 'keep everything else the same,' and repeat the preserve list on each iteration to reduce drift." For several inputs: "Reference each input by index and description". For bodies: "full body visible, feet included". For photorealism: include the word "photorealistic" and ask for real texture ("pores, wrinkles, fabric wear, imperfections"). **[READ]** — [OpenAI cookbook, image models prompting guide](https://github.com/openai/openai-cookbook/blob/main/examples/multimodal/image-gen-models-prompting-guide.ipynb)
- The same guide's character-consistency workflow is two steps: a "character anchor" image that locks "appearance, proportions, outfit, and tone", then edits that pass that image back with a "Character Consistency" list and "Do not redesign the character". **[READ]** — [OpenAI cookbook, image models prompting guide](https://github.com/openai/openai-cookbook/blob/main/examples/multimodal/image-gen-models-prompting-guide.ipynb)
- Community prompts for the first Nano Banana (posts from August 2025): "Three views (front, side, back)" inside a character-design request, and "Generate the Front, Rear, Left, Right, Top, Bottom views on white. Evenly spaced. Consistent subject. Isometric Perspective Equivalence." **[READ-2ND]** — [PicoTrex/Awesome-Nano-Banana-images](https://github.com/PicoTrex/Awesome-Nano-Banana-images)
- A two-pass method is described for Nano Banana: make a photoreal portrait first, then feed it in for a four-angle turnaround plus close-ups; the locked portrait is named as the step that prevents consistency problems. **[EXTRACT]** — [invideo FAQ](https://invideo.io/faq/how-do-you-create-a-character-sheet-for-ai-video/)

**One sheet or one view per generation**
- "A combined sheet is a convenient first attempt because you can compare several views together. It can also produce different proportions or ignore a requested angle." The advice that follows is to correct a bad view against the master instead of regenerating the whole design. **[EXTRACT]** — [diyai.io](https://diyai.io/ai-tools/image-generation/ai-character-sheet/)
- A third-party skill generates front, three-quarter, side and back as four separate calls with the same description and stitches them afterwards. **[READ-2ND]** — [inference-sh/skills, character-design-sheet](https://github.com/inference-sh/skills/blob/main/guides/design/character-design-sheet/SKILL.md)
- OpenAI states that above 2560x1440 results are "experimental"; a four-view sheet in one image therefore leaves each figure well under 1K wide. **[READ]** — [openai 3.55.1 on PyPI](https://pypi.org/project/openai/3.55.1/)

**Common artefacts and how they are caught**
- Checklists from practitioner guides: "Bag, scar, tattoo or logo on the same side in every view"; "For a character with deliberate asymmetry, inspect whether that asymmetry is preserved or accidentally mirrored"; "Line the frames up and look for drift: a jaw that widens, a costume detail that appears in one view and vanishes in the next"; "The side view should match the height and depth cues implied by the front view." **[EXTRACT]** — [diyai.io](https://diyai.io/ai-tools/image-generation/ai-character-sheet/); [Figma resource library](https://www.figma.com/resource-library/ai-character-consistency/); [getimg.ai](https://getimg.ai/blog/how-to-make-a-character-reference-sheet-with-ai)
- fal's skill gives a rejection list: "Face shape, eye spacing, hairstyle, marks, or body build drift. Outfit changes when the prompt says only expression or pose should change. The sheet mixes styles across panels." **[READ]** — [fal-ai-community/skills, character-design](https://github.com/fal-ai-community/skills/blob/main/skills/character-design/SKILL.md)
- Left and right are defined differently by different tools. Tripo: "Left view (90°), i.e. the subject's left side", "Back view (180°)", "Right view (270°), i.e. the subject's right side". fal's multiple-angles endpoint: "0°=front view, 90°=right side, 180°=back view, 270°=left side". **[READ]** — [ComfyUI `nodes_tripo.py`](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_tripo.py); [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fal-ai/client/v/1.11.0)

**A-pose or T-pose**
- The 3D generators treat both as valid and offer a switch: Meshy has `pose_mode` "a-pose" / "t-pose" / none; Rodin has `TAPose` ("Generate characters in T-pose or A-pose format, making them easier to rig and animate in 3D software"). **[READ]** — [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fal-ai/client/v/1.11.0); [ComfyUI `nodes_rodin.py`](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_rodin.py)
- fal's own 3D character recipe starts from a "1024x1536 character full-body T-pose" image and sends it to Meshy with rigging enabled. One vendor template uses a neutral A-pose for the sheet; another guide starts "from your front-facing character in a T-pose". **[READ]** / **[EXTRACT]** — [fal-ai-community/skills, fal-regenerate-3d](https://github.com/fal-ai-community/skills/blob/main/skills/fal-regenerate-3d/SKILL.md); [3D AI Studio template](https://www.3daistudio.com/templates/app/character-turnaround-sheet); [Scenario help](https://help.scenario.com/en/articles/use-multi-view-for-better-3d-models)
- Meshy's web documentation says pose controls apply to humanoid characters only and that a custom pose setting overrides the stance in the input image. **[EXTRACT]** — [Meshy docs, Image to 3D](https://docs.meshy.ai/en/webapp/image-to-3d)

**Dedicated multi-view models**
- Era3D (NeurIPS 2024; before mid-2025): six views at 512 pixels. An orthographic variant (October 2024) keeps the input and the generated front view aligned but "is only suitable for images with minimal perspective distortions". Licence AGPL-3.0, "so any downstream solution and products that include our codes or the pretrained model inside it should be open-sourced". **[READ]** — [pengHTYX/Era3D README](https://github.com/pengHTYX/Era3D)
- MV-Adapter (ICCV 2025; code from late 2024, last update June 2025): a plug-in adapter that turns SDXL or SD 2.1 into a multi-view generator at 768 pixels; image-to-multiview with a `--remove_bg` flag; its ground-truth dataset has 10 orthographic views at 1024x1024. **[READ]** — [huanngzh/MV-Adapter README](https://github.com/huanngzh/MV-Adapter)
- CharacterGen (SIGGRAPH 2024; before mid-2025): "Efficient 3D Character Generation from Single Images with Multi-View Pose Calibration", trained on an anime dataset of VRM characters (Anime3D). **[READ]** — [zjp-shadow/CharacterGen README](https://github.com/zjp-shadow/CharacterGen)
- PSHuman (2024; before mid-2025): "Photorealistic Single-image 3D Human Reconstruction using Cross-Scale Multiview Diffusion"; the model is trained at 768 pixels and needs over 40 GB of VRAM; it builds on SMPL-X. **[READ]** — [pengHTYX/PSHuman README](https://github.com/pengHTYX/PSHuman)
- Camera-control LoRAs on current editing models: ComfyUI documents a template that pairs Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 with a multiple-angles LoRA: "Load one photo, orbit a 3D camera around the subject, and Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 renders that same subject from the angle you picked." **[READ]** — [Comfy-Org/docs, qwen-image-edit-2511.mdx](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/docs/blob/main/tutorials/image/qwen/qwen-image-edit-2511.mdx)
- That LoRA (published by fal) is described as covering 96 camera positions and trained on 3,000+ Gaussian-splatting renders. One Japanese user note says the success rate with a separate reference image is very low. No source found confirms that side and back views of a human stay identity-consistent. **[EXTRACT]** — [Hugging Face, fal/Qwen-Image-Edit-2511-Multiple-Angles-LoRA](https://huggingface.co/fal/Qwen-Image-Edit-2511-Multiple-Angles-LoRA); [scrapbox note](https://scrapbox.io/work4ai/Qwen-Image-Edit-2511-Multiple-Angles-LoRA)

**Do image-to-3D tools generate the other views themselves**
- Tripo has an explicit step: "Tripo: Image to Multiview" "Generates front, left, back and right views of the subject", and "Tripo: Edit Multiview" "Edits the views of a Tripo: Image to Multiview result with per-view text instructions." **[READ]** — [ComfyUI `nodes_tripo.py`](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_tripo.py)
- Hunyuan3D-2mv (18 March 2025) is a multi-view shape model, but "multiple view images are not mandatory in this workflow - you can use only the `front` view image to generate a 3D model." **[READ]** — [Comfy-Org/docs, hunyuan3D-2.mdx](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/docs/blob/main/tutorials/3d/hunyuan3D-2.mdx)
- Every generator below also has a single-image endpoint (Tripo, Meshy, Rodin, Hunyuan3D, TRELLIS.2, Hi3D). **[READ]** — [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fal-ai/client/v/1.11.0)
- TRELLIS's multi-image conditioning (December 2024) "is based on tuning-free algorithm without training a specialized model, so it may not give the best results for all input images." **[READ]** — [microsoft/TRELLIS README](https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS)
- Body shape without any sheet: SAM 3D Body (Meta, checkpoints 19 Nov 2025) "can reconstruct 3D full-body human mesh from a single image", estimating body, feet and hands on the Momentum Human Rig; Hunyuan3D-Omni (25 Sep 2025) adds skeleton pose control to image-to-3D. **[READ]** — [facebookresearch/sam-3d-body README](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam-3d-body); [Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-Omni README](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-Omni)

### Inferences
- "Orthographic" cannot be had from these image models by a setting; it is only a word in the prompt. For reconstruction, the practical substitute is a long-lens look ("telephoto, minimal perspective distortion, camera at chest height") and the same framing in every view. Era3D's note that its orthographic variant only suits inputs with little perspective distortion supports asking for that.
- Because the two angle conventions above are mirror images of each other, any automated pipeline needs one deliberate asymmetric marker on the character (a mole, a watch, a side parting) and a check that it sits on the subject's correct side in every view; without it a mirrored profile cannot be detected.
- One view per generation at 1024x1536 or 2K each gives four to eight times the pixels per view of a combined sheet, and lets one bad view be redone alone. The combined sheet remains useful as an extra reference image, because it carries the proportions of all views in one frame.
- Flat, even light and a plain mid-grey or white background follow from what the 3D tools ask for (section 3) and from the "delight" and "de-shading" switches they expose: baked shadows are treated as a defect to be removed.
- The pre-2025 multi-view models are of little use for a photoreal human at production resolution (512 to 768 pixels, object-centric or anime training data, restrictive licences), except PSHuman's idea of conditioning on a body model.

### Gaps
- Zero123-family models were not looked at (no source opened); they are older than mid-2025.
- "Sheet" LoRAs for FLUX or SDXL (character-sheet LoRAs on Civitai) could not be researched: the hosts were not reachable and no search extract covered them.
- No source compares one-sheet against per-view generation with numbers.
- No source found describes a tested pipeline from Qwen multiple-angles views into a 3D generator for a human.

## 3. What image-to-3D tools want as input, by their own documentation

### Takeaway
All of them want one subject, a removed or plain background and a front view first; multi-view endpoints take 2 to 5 images (Tripo: front, left, back, right; Meshy: 1 to 4; Rodin: up to 5; Hunyuan3D 3.1: front plus up to seven more including 45° views; Hi3D: four), and several can force an A- or T-pose themselves. So a sheet of front, left, back and right full-body views on a clean background, each as its own image, fits every tool; three-quarter views are used only by Hunyuan3D 3.1 and Rodin.

### Cited Findings
- **Tripo** (versions seen: `v3.1-20260211`, P1, `P2-20260801`): multiview takes "2 to 4 image URLs of the same object from different angles. Order: [front, left, back, right]. Front view is required." Options: `orientation: align_image`, `texture_alignment: original_image | geometry`, `auto_size` ("Auto-scale the model to real-world dimensions (meters)"), quad output as FBX; P2 has `delight` ("Remove baked-in lighting before texturing"). **[READ]** — [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fal-ai/client/v/1.11.0); [ComfyUI `nodes_tripo.py`](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_tripo.py); [VAST-AI-Research/tripo-python-sdk README](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/tripo-python-sdk)
- Tripo rigging: "v1.0: humanoid (biped) characters only, 90+ animation presets"; bone naming "Tripo native or Mixamo-compatible". **[READ]** — [ComfyUI `nodes_tripo.py`](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_tripo.py)
- **Meshy** (versions seen: `meshy-6`, `meshy-7`, `meshy-7.1`): multi-image takes "1 to 4 images for 3D model creation. All images should depict the same object from different angles. Supports .jpg, .jpeg, .png formats"; "Multi-image generation does not support 4k"; `pose_mode`, `symmetry_mode` (off / auto / on), `topology` quad or triangle. The ComfyUI node requires at least 2 images. **[READ]** — [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fal-ai/client/v/1.11.0); [ComfyUI `nodes_meshy.py`](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_meshy.py)
- Meshy rigging "is currently not suitable for untextured meshes, non-humanoid assets, or humanoid assets with unclear limb and body structure"; it takes a height in metres (default 1.7). **[READ]** — [ComfyUI `nodes_meshy.py`](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_meshy.py)
- Meshy's help centre advises removing the background from every view, not only the primary one; an older Meshy blog post (Meshy-5, May 2025) recommends a front-facing character in a neutral pose, a background-free image, images over 1040x1040 pixels and a single subject. **[EXTRACT]** — [Meshy help centre](https://intercom.help/meshy/en/articles/9996860-how-to-use-meshy-image-to-3d); [Meshy blog](https://www.meshy.ai/blog/meshy-5-image-to-3d)
- **Rodin Gen-2.5** (Hyper3D): "1-5 images. The first image is used for materials when multi-view."; `TAPose` "for human-like models"; `use_original_alpha` "preserves the transparency channel from input images"; `texture_delight` "Removes baked lighting/highlights from the generated textures"; tiers from Extreme-Low to Extreme-High, up to 2M triangles. **[READ]** — [ComfyUI `nodes_rodin.py`](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_rodin.py); [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fal-ai/client/v/1.11.0)
- **Hunyuan3D 3.1 Pro** (Tencent): front view image "Resolution: 128-5000px, max 8MB, formats: JPG/PNG/WEBP. Tips: simple background, single object, object >50% of frame." Optional back, left and right views; exclusive to 3.1: top, bottom, left-front 45° and right-front 45° views. Face count 40,000 to 1,500,000. **[READ]** — [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fal-ai/client/v/1.11.0)
- ComfyUI's Hunyuan3D node offers versions 3.0 and 3.1, a required image of at least 128x128 and optional left, right and back images. **[READ]** — [ComfyUI `nodes_hunyuan3d.py`](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_hunyuan3d.py)
- Open Hunyuan3D-2 / 2.1: the ComfyUI tutorial uses front, left, back and right inputs and notes "the input images have already been preprocessed to remove excess background". Hunyuan3D-2.1 needs 10 GB VRAM for shape and 29 GB for shape plus texture. **[READ]** — [Comfy-Org/docs, hunyuan3D-2.mdx](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/docs/blob/main/tutorials/3d/hunyuan3D-2.mdx); [Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1 README](https://github.com/Tencent-Hunyuan/Hunyuan3D-2.1)
- **Hi3D v3.0** (Hitem3D): front, back, left and right images, each PNG / JPEG / WebP up to 20 MB; a `shading` value for "de-shading strength"; the partner recommends 2,000,000 or 5,000,000 faces. **[READ]** — [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fal-ai/client/v/1.11.0)
- **TRELLIS.2** (Microsoft, 4B parameters, MIT licence): single image to 3D with PBR materials; on fal the resolution is 512, 1024 or 1536 and textures go to 4096. **[READ]** — [microsoft/TRELLIS.2 README](https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2); [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fal-ai/client/v/1.11.0)
- fal's own tips for image-to-3D: "Single object on plain background"; "Remove the background first if the source has clutter"; "Multiple angles help when the model supports multi-image input, front, side, three-quarter views give the best mesh"; "Generation is slow (1-5 minutes), always run async". **[READ]** — [fal-ai-community/skills, image-to-3d.md](https://github.com/fal-ai-community/skills/blob/main/skills/fal-models-catalog/references/image-to-3d.md)

### Inferences
- A sheet that serves every tool: four separate full-body images (front, subject's left, back, subject's right), the same pose and framing in all, background removed to alpha or flat, the figure filling well over half the frame, 1024 to 2048 pixels on the long side, PNG. Add left-front and right-front 45° views if Hunyuan3D 3.1 is a candidate.
- Since Rodin takes materials from the first image and Tripo requires the front view first, the front image should be the best one, with the most neutral light.
- Head turnaround and expression sheet are not inputs to any of these body endpoints; they serve the human artist, a separate head reconstruction, or texture and blendshape work.

### Gaps
- The providers' own documentation pages (Tripo, Meshy, Rodin, Tencent, Hitem3D) were not opened; the limits above come from the API field comments that fal and ComfyUI ship, which are close to but not the same as the providers' pages.
- No provider statement found on a recommended input resolution beyond Hunyuan3D's range and the old Meshy figure.
- No provider statement found on whether a transparent background or a flat colour is preferred, except Rodin's alpha option.

## 4. Variants on one identity: hairstyle, hair colour, skin tone, eye colour, age, expression

### Takeaway
All leading editors can change one attribute from a reference image with a "change only X, keep everything else" prompt, and the vendors show hairstyle grids and expression transfers as showcase cases, but the measured evidence says attribute edits move the face: a 2024 study found hair and other attribute edits degrade face recognition even with identity-preserving methods, and 2026 benchmarks still report identity drift. No source compares doing variants in 2D against doing them on the 3D asset; by the nature of the change, colour changes belong on the asset and shape changes of the hair need a new image.

### Cited Findings
- OpenAI's edit pattern locks the person explicitly: "Do not change her face, facial features, skin tone, body shape, pose, or identity in any way. Preserve her exact likeness, expression, hairstyle, and proportions. Replace only the clothing". **[READ]** — [OpenAI cookbook, image models prompting guide](https://github.com/openai/openai-cookbook/blob/main/examples/multimodal/image-gen-models-prompting-guide.ipynb)
- fal's skill: "Expression sheet: one approved face reference, multiple controlled expression prompts", or as one image: "expression sheet with nine head-and-shoulder portraits: neutral, happy, angry, afraid, sad, surprised, suspicious, determined, amused; same face, same hairstyle, same style, clean grid, no identity drift". For edits it routes to "`fal-ai/nano-banana-pro/edit`, then `openai/gpt-image-2/edit`, then `fal-ai/bytedance/seedream/v5/lite/edit`". **[READ]** — [fal-ai-community/skills, character-design](https://github.com/fal-ai-community/skills/blob/main/skills/character-design/SKILL.md); [prompt-patterns.md](https://github.com/fal-ai-community/skills/blob/main/skills/character-design/references/prompt-patterns.md)
- Community cases for Nano Banana (August 2025): "Generate avatars of this person with different hairstyles in a 3x3 grid format", and "Character reference from Image 1 / Change to the expression from Image 2". **[READ-2ND]** — [PicoTrex/Awesome-Nano-Banana-images](https://github.com/PicoTrex/Awesome-Nano-Banana-images)
- The same repository presents "Nano-consistent-150k", a dataset of over 150k Nano Banana samples "uniquely designed to preserve consistent human identity across diverse and complex editing scenarios". **[READ-2ND]** — [PicoTrex/Awesome-Nano-Banana-images](https://github.com/PicoTrex/Awesome-Nano-Banana-images)
- A 2024 study found that "facial attribute editing using modern generative AI models can severely degrade automated face recognition systems", that the degradation persists with identity-preserving methods, and it includes hair colour and hairstyle among the edits (older than mid-2025). **[EXTRACT]** — [arXiv 2403.08092](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2403.08092v1)
- Hair-transfer research measures identity with a face verifier (ArcFace). **[EXTRACT]** — [arXiv 2206.08585, HairFIT](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.08585)
- Masked edits keep the rest of the image by construction: OpenAI's edit takes a mask ("White marks regions to edit and black marks regions to keep" in fal's wrapper); Ideogram 4.5's high-precision edit works "with unchanged-pixel restoration" and "Localized edits preserve the original image resolution and every pixel outside the region." **[READ]** — [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fal-ai/client/v/1.11.0)
- Variants after reconstruction are also offered by the 3D tools: Tripo "Edit Multiview" with per-view text instructions, Meshy "Texture Model" and retexture endpoints (`meshy/v7/retexture`), and `fal-ai/trellis-2/retexture`. **[READ]** — [ComfyUI `nodes_tripo.py`](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_tripo.py); [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fal-ai/client/v/1.11.0)

### Inferences
- A 3x3 grid of hairstyles in one image is a browsing aid, not a reference: each cell is about a third of the frame and each face is generated again. For variants that will be used, generate each from the master at full size, with the hair region masked where the endpoint allows it, so that the face pixels are literally untouched.
- Hair colour, skin tone and eye colour change no geometry. On a 3D asset they are material parameters or texture edits and cost nothing in consistency; producing them in 2D is only useful as look references for the artist.
- Hairstyle changes the silhouette and belongs in 2D first; in a pipeline where hair is its own asset, the 2D hairstyle variants are references for hair authoring, and the head underneath should be reconstructed once (ideally from a version with the hair tied back or a cap, so the skull is visible).
- Age and expression change the face surface itself. Expression images are references for blendshapes, not for a new reconstruction per expression; age variants are a new identity for reconstruction purposes and need their own check.
- Reliability order from easiest to hardest, as far as the sources allow a judgement: hair or eye colour, expression, hairstyle, skin tone, age. This order is my inference; no source ranks them.

### Gaps
- No 2026 benchmark found that scores hairstyle, hair-colour, skin-tone, eye-colour or age edits for face drift on the current models.
- No source found on whether variants are better made in 2D before reconstruction or on the 3D asset afterwards.
- Skin-tone edits raise their own failure mode (the model changing facial features along with tone); no source was found on it.

## 5. Nude or underwear-level anatomical reference for a body model

### Takeaway
No provider policy page could be read. From extracts: the big hosted providers prohibit sexually explicit output, none of the texts seen draws a clear line for non-sexual anatomical nudity, and in practice Google's image safety filter has blocked even ordinary e-commerce underwear images; the APIs expose only coarse moderation dials. The legitimate routes are a sportswear or underwear-level base outfit, a neutral mannequin or base-mesh look, taking body shape from a body model instead of from skin, and locally run open-weight models under their licences.

### Cited Findings
- OpenAI's image API has one dial: `moderation`: "Control the content-moderation level for images generated by the GPT image models. Must be either `low` for less restrictive filtering or `auto` (default value)." ComfyUI's node sends `moderation="low"`. **[READ]** — [openai 3.55.1 on PyPI, `image_generate_params.py`](https://pypi.org/project/openai/3.55.1/); [ComfyUI `nodes_openai.py`](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_openai.py)
- A third-party article says OpenAI's policy forbids pornography, deepfake sexual content and non-consensual or underage nudity. An OpenAI community thread asks for "context-aware nudity filtering", saying the filter leans on visible skin; that is a user complaint, not policy. **[EXTRACT]** — [CometAPI article](https://www.cometapi.com/can-gpt-image-1-make-nsfw); [OpenAI community thread](https://community.openai.com/t/context-aware-nudity-filtering-needed-nordic-and-indigenous-examples/1389116)
- Google: an aggregator says the Generative AI Prohibited Use Policy "explicitly bans pornography, erotic content for sexual gratification, non-consensual intimate imagery", that an image-safety filter flags nudity, and that filters on Nano Banana 2 cannot be fully turned off. **[EXTRACT]** — [CometAPI article](https://www.cometapi.com/how-to-use-nano-banana-2-to-generate-nsfw-images)
- Developers report on Google's forum that Nano Banana Pro blocked "non-NSFW ecommerce underwear images with IMAGE_SAFETY error" and "normal fashion use case" images. **[EXTRACT]** — [Google AI developers forum thread 113109](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/nano-banana-pro-suddenly-blocking-non-nsfw-ecommerce-underwear-images-with-image-safety-error/113109); [thread 118922](https://discuss.ai.google.dev/t/possible-regression-in-image-safety-filtering-for-nano-banana-pro-affecting-normal-fashion-use-case/118922)
- fal exposes per-request dials: on the Gemini endpoints `safety_tolerance` "1" to "6" ("1 is the most strict … 6 is the least strict", default "4"); on FLUX.2 "1" to "5" (default "2"); on FLUX 3 0 to 4 (default 2). On many endpoints `enable_safety_checker` defaults to true and "Disabling it requires account authorization; unauthorized requests are always checked". **[READ]** — [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fal-ai/client/v/1.11.0)
- BFL's direct FLUX 3 Image call has `safety_tolerance` 0 to 4, "0 is the strictest". **[READ]** — [ComfyUI `nodes_bfl.py`](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_bfl.py)
- Adobe: a secondary guide says Adobe's generative AI guidelines prohibit nudity; users report Generative Fill refusing to work on photos of nude models even to add clothing. **[EXTRACT]** — [Adobe community thread](https://community.adobe.com/questions-712/why-is-it-that-when-i-work-with-a-photo-of-a-nude-model-even-to-try-to-cover-them-up-more-generative-ai-does-not-generate-anything-in-the-selected-area-1553189)
- Stability AI: its acceptable use policy has prohibited sexually explicit content through hosted APIs since 31 July 2025, per a platform comparison. **[EXTRACT]** — [crepal.ai](https://crepal.ai/blog/aiimage/ai-image-generators-that-allow-nsfw/)
- Open weights that can be run locally and their licences: Qwen-Image family, Apache 2.0; FLUX.2 [klein] 4B, Apache 2.0; FLUX.2 [dev] and klein 9B, non-commercial. **[READ]** — [QwenLM/Qwen-Image README](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen-Image); [black-forest-labs/flux2 README](https://github.com/black-forest-labs/flux2)
- Body shape without skin: SAM 3D Body recovers a full-body mesh with body-model parameters from a single (clothed) image. **[READ]** — [facebookresearch/sam-3d-body README](https://github.com/facebookresearch/sam-3d-body)

### Inferences
- For a body model the sheet does not need nudity. What reconstruction needs is the silhouette and the surface where it will be seen. Fitted sportswear (sports bra and shorts, or briefs) in a plain colour shows the silhouette; given the Google reports, "fitted athletic wear" is a safer wording than "underwear".
- A neutral "anatomical mannequin / grey base mesh, no genitalia, no nipples, smooth matte surface" look is a second route for proportions; it reads as an object, not a person, and gives a clean geometry reference, though no skin.
- Since refusals are filter decisions that change without notice (the forum threads describe a regression), a pipeline should treat a blocked request as a normal outcome, retry with a more clothed wording, and never try to slip nudity past a filter.
- Skin for the covered regions is better taken from the project's existing skin shading work than from generated images.

### Gaps
- No provider's usage policy was read. What OpenAI, Google, Black Forest Labs, ByteDance, Midjourney and Ideogram each say about non-sexual nudity (figure drawing, anatomical reference) is not established here.
- Whether OpenAI's policy changed for adult content in late 2025 or 2026 was not found.
- What fal's "account authorization" for disabling the safety checker requires was not found.

## 6. Policies and law around real people; ownership and commercial use; provenance and indemnity

### Takeaway
Only extracts were available for this whole question. They say: OpenAI prohibits using someone's photorealistic likeness without consent in ways that could confuse authenticity; Google restricts public figures and face or outfit swaps on real people; the EU AI Act's transparency duties (Article 50) have applied since 2 August 2026 with a voluntary code of practice built on machine-readable marking such as C2PA; likeness law is moving (Denmark's bill, the stalled US NO FAKES Act); under US practice a purely generated image is not copyrightable; and indemnity exists only on specific enterprise products (Adobe Firefly's own models, Google's listed cloud services, OpenAI's API and enterprise tiers, Getty). Nothing found addresses an invented face that happens to resemble a real person.

### Cited Findings

**Real people**

### Inferences
- An invented person from a text description alone touches none of the real-person rules; the risk is accidental resemblance. Because the Danish text and the publicity-law tradition turn on a person being imitated or identifiable, the defensible position is a record that shows no real person was the source and that resemblance was looked for.
- If reference photographs of real people are used as input (even to "blend" several), the likeness and consent rules above apply to the input, and the provider's filters may refuse. For an invented person the clean route is text only, or references that are themselves generated.
- What a project should keep per character, so that the origin can be shown later: the full prompt text and anchor; every input image with its own origin; provider, model id and snapshot, date and request id for every generated image; the provider terms and policy version in force that day; the result of a resemblance check (for instance a reverse image search on the master portrait) with date; any C2PA or watermark data left intact in the stored originals; and who approved the master.
- Going through fal (or any aggregator) puts a second contract between the project and the model provider. The provider-level indemnities above are tied to the provider's own enterprise products, so they should be assumed not to apply to calls made through fal unless fal's terms say otherwise; the quoted fal terms point the other way.
- Since a purely generated sheet is probably not copyrightable in the US, the sheet itself is weak property; the 3D asset that human artists build from it carries the authorship. That is an argument for treating the sheet as working reference and for recording the human work.

- No terms or policy page was read: OpenAI, Google, Black Forest Labs, Midjourney, Ideogram, Adobe, ByteDance and fal all remain to be checked at the source. The exact current wording on output ownership per provider is not established.
- Accidental resemblance of an invented face to a real person: no source found, either legal or practical.

- German and EU personality-rights law (relevant for a project based in Europe) was not researched beyond the AI Act.
- ByteDance's terms and data-handling for Seedream: nothing found.

- Open reward models for edits exist: "EditScore — a family of state-of-the-art open-source reward models (7B–72B) for instruction-guided image editing" (30 Sep 2025). **[READ]** — [VectorSpaceLab/OmniGen2 README](https://github.com/VectorSpaceLab/OmniGen2)

**Claude's limits as the judge**
- Anthropic's vision documentation: up to 100 images per API request; with more than 20 images in a request each is limited to 2000x2000 pixels; images over 8000x8000 are rejected; Claude will not identify (name) people in images; its spatial reasoning is limited and it "may struggle with tasks needing precise localization or layout"; it cannot reliably tell whether an image is AI-generated; counts are approximate. **[EXTRACT]** — [Anthropic docs, Vision](https://platform.claude.com/docs/build-with-claude/vision)
- High-resolution vision: maximum image resolution of 2,576 pixels on the long edge (about 3.75 MP) since Opus 4.7, up to about 4,784 visual tokens per image; coordinates map 1:1 to image pixels. Read in Anthropic's `claude-api` skill bundled with Claude Code, not on the documentation site. **[READ]** — [Anthropic docs, Vision](https://platform.claude.com/docs/build-with-claude/vision)
- Current Claude prices per million tokens in the same bundled skill (cached 3 Nov 2026): Opus 5.5 4 / 20 USD, Sonnet 5.5 2 / 10 USD, Haiku 5.5 0.10 / 0.50 USD, Fable 5.2 8 / 40 USD (input / output). **[READ]** — [Anthropic docs, Vision](https://platform.claude.com/docs/build-with-claude/vision)
- ComfyUI's Claude node caps a call at 20 images and 1568x1568 pixels each. **[READ]** — [ComfyUI `nodes_anthropic.py`](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_anthropic.py)

### Inferences
- What Claude does well in this loop: writing and keeping the anchor and the per-view prompts; checking each output against a written list (is it the requested view, is the whole body in frame, is the outfit unchanged, is the background plain, is the pose the same, are there extra fingers or props); comparing a new view side by side with the master; and deciding whether to retry, with what change to the prompt.
- Where it needs support: fine identity (the same nose, the same eye spacing) across a front and a profile view, where even people disagree; left/right, given the documented limit in spatial reasoning; and proportions between views. Three supports follow from the sources: crop the face of each view and compare at full resolution (the cookbook's crop tool); put a deliberate asymmetric marker on the character and ask only "on which side of the image is the marker"; and measure proportions with code (segmentation mask height, head height, shoulder width in pixels) instead of by eye.
- A face-embedding similarity (ArcFace-style) would give a number for front and three-quarter views and is what the hair-editing literature uses; it works poorly on profiles and not at all on back views, and the common open face models carry non-commercial terms (as InstantID's README warns), so it is an aid for internal checking only.
- Claude comparing two generated faces of an invented person is not "identifying a person"; the documented refusal concerns naming real people. With reference photographs of real people the judge prompt should be about similarity of features, never about who it is.
- At about 4,800 image tokens per full-resolution image, judging one new view against a master and three earlier views is about 24,000 input tokens: about 0.10 USD on Opus 5.5 and 0.05 USD on Sonnet 5.5, the same order as generating the image.
- A human gate remains necessary at two points: approving the master (taste and resemblance), and approving the finished sheet. None of the published workflows claims to remove it.

### Gaps
- No published measurement found of how well any vision-language model, Claude included, judges face identity across views, or detects mirrored views.
- No published agent workflow found that closes the loop with a measured identity score.
- The Anthropic documentation site was not opened; the per-request limits above are from a search extract.

## 10. A fal-only character sheet recipe

### Takeaway
A workable fal-only sequence, assembled from the sources above and not yet tested: write the anchor; make one master portrait and approve it by a human; derive a full-body front view; derive each further view as its own edit with the master, the front view and all approved views as references; check every output with Claude against a written list before it may serve as a reference; cut backgrounds; then hand front, left, back and right to a multi-view 3D endpoint. First choices for the turnaround: `openai/gpt-image-2.5/sunburst/edit` (16 references, masks, native transparency, about 0.04 USD at high quality) and `fal-ai/nano-banana-pro/edit` or `google/nano-banana-2.1/edit` (14 references); which of them holds this particular face best has to be found by running both on the first two views.

### Cited Findings
- Endpoint ids, input fields and limits used below: **[READ]** — [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fal-ai/client/v/1.11.0). Prices: **[READ-2ND]** — [LiteLLM price table](https://github.com/BerriAI/litellm/blob/main/model_prices_and_context_window.json).
- fal's own routing puts `openai/gpt-image-2` first for consistent characters and `fal-ai/nano-banana-pro/edit` first for edits; that guidance predates the 2.5 endpoints in the client. **[READ]** — [fal-ai-community/skills, model-routing](https://github.com/fal-ai-community/skills/blob/main/skills/model-routing/SKILL.md)
- Anchor and prompt patterns, the rejection list, and "one approved face reference, multiple controlled expression prompts": **[READ]** — [fal-ai-community/skills, character-design](https://github.com/fal-ai-community/skills/blob/main/skills/character-design/SKILL.md)
- "Change only X" wording, references by index, and the transparent-background wording: **[READ]** — [OpenAI cookbook, image models prompting guide](https://github.com/openai/openai-cookbook/blob/main/examples/multimodal/image-gen-models-prompting-guide.ipynb)
- Judge rubric (facial similarity and body shape 0 to 5, fail at 2 or below): **[READ]** — [OpenAI cookbook, image_evals.ipynb](https://github.com/openai/openai-cookbook/blob/main/examples/multimodal/image_evals.ipynb)
- Angle conventions (Tripo: 90° is the subject's left; fal multiple-angles: 90° is the right side): **[READ]** — [ComfyUI `nodes_tripo.py`](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_tripo.py); [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@fal-ai/client/v/1.11.0)

### Inferences

The sequence. Every step is a fal call unless marked otherwise; `→` gives what the step produces.

1. **Anchor (no call).** Write the immutable anchor (face, eyes, brows, nose, mouth, skin marks, hair, build, base outfit) and add one deliberate asymmetric marker, for example a mole on the subject's left cheek. Base outfit: plain fitted sportswear, barefoot. → `anchor.txt`
2. **Master portrait.** `openai/gpt-image-2.5/sunburst/text-to-image` (or `google/nano-banana-2.1`), 3 to 4 candidates: `prompt` = anchor + "photorealistic head-and-shoulders portrait, neutral expression, mouth closed, looking into the camera, even soft frontal light, no makeup, plain mid-grey background, real skin texture with pores"; quality `high`; portrait size. If the person is to be derived from reference photographs, use the `/edit` endpoint with those photographs in `image_urls` instead, and the consent questions of section 6 apply. A human picks one. → `master.png`
3. **Full-body front.** `openai/gpt-image-2.5/sunburst/edit`: `image_urls: [master]`; `prompt` = anchor + "Image 1 is the face reference. Full-body photograph of the same person, front view, standing in a relaxed A-pose with arms about 30 degrees from the body, palms towards the thighs, feet shoulder-width apart, entire body visible including feet and hands, camera at chest height with a long lens, even flat light, no cast shadow, plain mid-grey background. Do not change the face, hair or body proportions."; `image_size` 1024x1536 or larger; `quality: "high"`. → `body_front.png`
4. **Check (Claude, not fal).** Compare `body_front` with `master`: face crop side by side; pose as asked; whole body in frame; marker on the subject's left. Reject and retry, or approve. The same check follows every step below.
5. **Body views, one call each.** Same endpoint; `image_urls: [master, body_front, …all approved views]`; prompt names the view by the subject's side: "left profile view: the camera sees the subject's left side, the subject faces the left edge of the image" (then the marker is visible), "back view", "right profile view" (marker not visible), and the two front three-quarter views. Repeat the full preserve list each time. → `body_left.png`, `body_back.png`, `body_right.png`, `body_34_left.png`, `body_34_right.png`
   - Alternative for a view that keeps failing: `fal-ai/qwen-image-edit-2511-multiple-angles` with `image_urls: [body_front]` and `horizontal_angle` 90, 180 or 270, then verify which side came out, because its 90° is the opposite side to Tripo's.
6. **Head turnaround.** Same edit endpoint, head-and-shoulders framing, five to eight views (front, both three-quarters, both profiles, optionally back, from slightly above and below); `image_urls: [master, the matching body view, approved head views]`. For the skull shape add one set with the hair tied back. → `head_*.png`
7. **Expression sheet.** One call per expression from `master`: "Change only the facial expression to [a closed-mouth smile | a broad smile showing teeth | surprise | anger | sadness | fear | disgust | eyes closed, neutral | jaw open, neutral]. Keep the head position, hair, lighting, framing and identity exactly the same." → `expr_*.png`
8. **Hairstyle and colour variants.** Mask the hair region of `master` and of the head views (`fal-ai/sam-3/image`), then `openai/gpt-image-2.5/sunburst/edit` with `mask_url` (white marks the region to edit) and "Change only the hair to [style, length, colour]. Do not change the face." Where the new hair is larger than the old mask, use an unmasked edit on `fal-ai/nano-banana-pro/edit` and check the face crop. `ideogram/v4.5/edit` with `edit_precision: "high"` and a mask is the second masked option. Eye and skin colour: as look references only (section 4). → `hair_*.png`
9. **Background.** Either ask for `background: "transparent"` with PNG output in steps 3 to 8, or run `fal-ai/birefnet/v2` (model "General Use (Heavy)" or "Portrait", `operating_resolution: "2048x2048"`) or `fal-ai/bria/background/remove` on the approved views. Check hair edges and the gaps between arms and body. → `*_alpha.png`
10. **Optional upscale.** `fal-ai/seedvr/upscale/image` or `topaz/upscale/image/transparent`, on approved views only, followed by the identity check again, since upscalers invent detail.
11. **Hand-over to 3D.** `tripo3d/h3.1/multiview-to-3d` with `image_urls: [body_front, body_left, body_back, body_right]` in exactly that order; or `meshy/v7.1/multi-image-to-3d` (up to 4 images, `pose_mode: "a-pose"`); or `fal-ai/hunyuan-3d/v3.1/pro/image-to-3d` (front plus back, left, right and the two 45° views); or `fal-ai/hyper3d/rodin/v2.5` (up to 5 images, `TAPose: true`, `use_original_alpha: true`). Optionally `fal-ai/sam-3/3d-body` on `body_front` for body-model parameters. The 3D step itself belongs to the other researchers' notes.
12. **Record.** For every image kept: endpoint id, full input, request id, date, and the check result (section 6).

Further points.
- Why the second model: running step 3 and one profile view on both `openai/gpt-image-2.5/sunburst/edit` and `fal-ai/nano-banana-pro/edit` (or `google/nano-banana-2.1/edit`) costs under 1 USD and decides the model for this face. Mixing models within one sheet is likely to mix skin rendering and should be avoided once the choice is made.
- Reference order matters where the model says so (FLUX 3: "The first controls auto aspect ratio"; Seedream: "only the last 10 are used"); keep the master first and the newest approved view last, and state each image's role in the prompt.
- Do not use a view as a reference before it has passed the check; an unnoticed drift is copied into every later view.
- Estimated cost of one clean pass (about 27 images at high quality on GPT Image 2.5, about 0.04 USD each, plus background removal): under 2 USD; with retries and the judge calls, 5 to 15 USD per character. This is arithmetic on the prices in section 9, not a measured figure.
- A later, stronger variant once a sheet exists: train a character LoRA on the approved views (`fal-ai/flux-2-trainer-v2`, `blackforestlabs/flux-3/klein/base/trainer` or `fal-ai/qwen-image-edit-2509-trainer`) and regenerate the views with it.

### Gaps
- The recipe has not been run. Which fal model holds a photoreal face best across profile and back views, and how many retries a view needs, are unknown until it is tried.
- Whether `background: "transparent"` on the GPT Image 2.5 edit endpoints gives clean hair edges on a full-body figure is untested; so is BiRefNet's "Portrait" model on full bodies.
- The arm angle and wording that the 3D generators and their riggers accept best as an "A-pose" is not documented by any of them.
- Limits of the mask route for hairstyles much larger than the original hair are untested.
