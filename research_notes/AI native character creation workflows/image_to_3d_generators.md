# AI image-to-3D and text-to-3D generators for human game characters (state on 18 November 2026)

**How to read these notes.** Every finding carries one of three marks:

- **[READ]** I opened the primary source myself (a repository file, a licence text, a published SDK or client package).
- **[EXTRACT]** I saw only what the web-search tool returned about the page (a summary or quoted fragment). The page itself could not be opened.
- **[SECONDARY]** The claim comes from a third-party review, blog or aggregator, and I saw it only as a search extract.

**What could and could not be opened.** From this environment only `github.com`, `raw.githubusercontent.com`, `pypi.org` and `registry.npmjs.org` resolve. Every vendor site (`tripo3d.ai`, `meshy.ai`, `hyper3d.ai`, `3daistudio.com`, `fal.ai`, `replicate.com`, `stability.ai`, `csm.ai`, `lumalabs.ai`), plus `huggingface.co`, `arxiv.org` and `reddit.com`, was refused by the network proxy. So no vendor terms of service, pricing page or documentation page was read directly. The primary sources I did read are, in the main: the vendors' own open-source SDKs and MCP servers on GitHub (Tripo's Python SDK 1.0.0 of October 2026, Meshy's MCP server 0.6.12 of 17 November 2026), fal's published JavaScript client 1.11.0 of 14 November 2026 (its typed endpoint map lists every 3D endpoint with its input and output schema), ComfyUI's partner-API node sources (which carry the parameters and price formulas ComfyUI charges for Tripo, Rodin, Hunyuan3D and Meshy), and the repositories and licence files of the open-weight models. 30 web searches were used; none returned a Reddit thread.

## 1. Which tools exist and lead as of November 2026, and what does each take in and put out?

### Takeaway
Four hosted families lead and all shipped new models in 2026: Tripo (H3.1 for fidelity, P2.0 for native quad meshes), Meshy (7.1), Hyper3D Rodin (Gen-2.5) and Tencent Hunyuan3D (3.1, hosted only); the leading model that can be self-hosted under a permissive licence is Microsoft TRELLIS.2 (MIT, single image only). CSM and Luma Genie appear to have left the field, Roblox Cube and Stability's models are not competitive for textured humans, and no 2026 image-to-3D product from Adobe, Autodesk or NVIDIA was found.

### Inferences
- "H3.1" in Tripo's marketing and on fal is the API model `v3.1-20260211`: ComfyUI defaults to that id, fal names its endpoints `tripo3d/h3.1/*`, and both appeared in the first quarter of 2026. Not confirmed by a Tripo page I could read.
- For a character sheet with front, side, back and three-quarter views, Hunyuan3D 3.1 Pro is the only hosted model whose read schema has dedicated three-quarter (45°) slots. Tripo and Hitem3D take four orthogonal views; Meshy takes up to four unlabelled images; Rodin takes up to five unlabelled images. TRELLIS.2 itself takes one image.
- Native output is triangles for every tool; "quad" is a requested mode (Tripo P2 native quad-dominant up to 25,000 faces; Rodin Quad up to 200K; Meshy quad remesh; Hunyuan "quadrilateral" low-poly or smart topology). Tripo delivers quad meshes as FBX, so a three.js pipeline that wants GLB must convert.
- Open weights have fallen behind hosted models at Tencent: the newest downloadable Hunyuan3D is 2.1 from June 2025, while the hosted line is at 3.1.

### Gaps
- No source found for a 2026 Stability, Adobe or Autodesk image-to-3D mesh product; one search covering all of them returned nothing relevant. Meta's AssetGen / 3D Gen did not surface as a usable product.
- Kaedim and Sloyd status in 2026 rests on listicles; a claimed Kaedim price of $20/month in one list looks unreliable and is not repeated above.
- Generation times for Rodin Gen-2.5, Hunyuan3D 3.1 and Meshy 7.1 were not found in a primary source (Rodin's "1 million polygons in 4 seconds" is a press claim: [dev.to summary](https://dev.to/gentic_news/deemostech-rodin-gen-25-10m-polygon-3d-genai-in-4-seconds-4pd9)).

## 2. Character-specific features: poses, rigging, faces, hands, hair, eyes and mouth

### Takeaway
T-pose/A-pose generation and automatic body rigging are standard (Meshy, Tripo, Rodin), but nothing I could read in any API offers facial blendshapes, separate eyeballs, teeth, a mouth interior or hair cards: the output is one fused, textured shell with a body skeleton at best.

### Cited Findings
- **[READ]** Meshy: `pose_mode` "'a-pose' or 't-pose'; use 't-pose' when the model will be rigged or animated" on text, image and multi-image generation — [src/schemas/generation.ts](https://github.com/meshy-dev/meshy-mcp-server/blob/main/src/schemas/generation.ts)

### Inferences
- Across the Tripo SDK, the Meshy MCP server, the ComfyUI Rodin and Hunyuan nodes and fal's 3D schemas, no parameter or output field mentions blendshapes, morph targets, facial rig, eyes, teeth, tongue, mouth cavity, eyelashes or hair cards. The rigs are body skeletons. A face that must talk, blink or be seen close therefore needs its animatable parts built or fitted separately.

- Symmetric topology is not something the generators guarantee: Tripo's `force_symmetry` is a conversion option and Meshy's symmetry switch is a no-op.

## 3. How good are the results for realistic humans, above all faces seen close?

### Takeaway
No independent 2025–2026 test of photoreal human heads at close range was found; what evidence exists says faces and hands are the weak point of every general tool, that roughly one generation in ten is usable without rework, and that the "best for humans" label most often attached to Rodin rests on vendor-adjacent listicles rather than measurement.

### Inferences
- The failure modes the brief lists are consistent with what was found, though mostly from secondary sources: softened or flattened faces (Meshy 6), a mouth with four teeth and no real interior (Rodin), guessed backs of heads, extra limbs and bad fingers on text-to-3D, dense triangle topology that deforms badly, internal duplicate walls (TRELLIS.2), and face distortion on full-body generations (Hunyuan3D 2). A full body generated in one pass gives the face a small share of the geometry and texture budget, which fits the unanswered Hunyuan issue.
- The dedicated human models that exist as open code are Gaussian-splat avatars, not meshes with PBR maps. They are fast and animatable but they are a different rendering path from a three.js mesh character and none of the hosted mesh generators is positioned as their equal for likeness.
- Because Meshy's own surface-detail alignment score is 59.8% for its newest model, fine facial detail should be expected to diverge from the reference even on the latest generation.

### Gaps
- No Reddit, 80.lv or CG Channel test of realistic human heads from these tools in 2025–2026 was returned by any search; reddit.com could not be opened. The search tool itself concluded that the only reliable comparison is to run the same portrait through each model.
- No evidence at all on eyes painted on versus modelled, texture seams, or baked shadows on faces specifically.
- No independent evaluation of Rodin Gen-2.5, Tripo P2, Meshy 7.1 or Hunyuan3D 3.1 on humans.
- No comparison of dedicated head generators (LAM, ChatAvatar, Hitem3D portrait) against the general tools.

## 4. APIs and automation: who has an API, what a request looks like, what it costs

### Takeaway
Tripo, Meshy, Rodin and Hunyuan3D all have task-based REST APIs (submit, get a task id, poll, download a signed URL) and are all resold through fal.ai; a textured character costs roughly $0.30 to $1.75 per generation depending on model and options, with native-quad and flagship models at the top of that range.

### Cited Findings

**Request shape**
- **[READ]** Tripo: `POST /v3/generation/image-to-model` (or `multiview-to-model`, `text-to-model`) returns a task id; `GET /v3/tasks/{task_id}` is polled until `success`, `failed`, `cancelled`, `banned` or `expired`; the result is a single signed `model_url` that expires. Uploads go to `POST /v3/files` (limit 150 MB). API keys start with `tsk_`. - **[READ]** Meshy: every create call returns `{task_id, status}`; output formats are fixed at creation; an API key (prefix `msy_`) "requires Pro plan or above"; base URL `https://api.meshy.ai`; the server handles HTTP 429 "Rate limit exceeded" — [meshy-mcp-server README](https://github.com/meshy-dev/meshy-mcp-server), [src/instructions.ts](https://github.com/meshy-dev/meshy-mcp-server/blob/main/src/instructions.ts)

## 7. How generated meshes are made usable, and whether the tools do it themselves

### Takeaway
The tools now offer the mechanical steps themselves — decimation or quad remeshing, UV unwrapping, de-lighting of the colour map, part splitting, body rigging — but practitioners still treat the output as a high-resolution source to be retopologised and baked, especially for a deforming face.

### Cited Findings

**What each tool does itself**
- **[READ]** Tripo: Smart Low Poly (500–20,000 faces, quad ≤10,000, with Smart UV on v3.0), quad output, Smart UV, segmentation and part completion, texture model v3.5 with `delight`, conversion with `bake`, `pack_uv`, `force_symmetry`, `texture_size`, and meshopt compression — [docs/API.md](https://github.com/VAST-AI-Research/tripo-python-sdk/blob/master/docs/API.md), [ComfyUI nodes_tripo.py](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_tripo.py)
- **[READ]** Meshy: remesh to quad or triangle at 100–300,000 faces or by adaptive decimation, UV unwrap (source GLB ≤40,000 faces), retexture from text, one image or 1–4 views with optional PBR, `remove_lighting` on Meshy 6 only, rigging limited to 300,000 faces ("if over, remesh to reduce polycount first") — [meshy-mcp-server README](https://github.com/meshy-dev/meshy-mcp-server), [src/schemas/postprocessing.ts](https://github.com/meshy-dev/meshy-mcp-server/blob/main/src/schemas/postprocessing.ts)
- **[READ]** Rodin: Quad mesh mode, `texture_delight`, `material: "Shaded"` defined as "baked lighting with no PBR maps" versus PBR — [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://registry.npmjs.org/@fal-ai/client/-/client-1.11.0.tgz)
- **[READ]** Hunyuan3D: smart topology (triangle or mixed quad), UV unfold for models under 30,000 faces, part splitting from FBX of at most 30,000 faces — [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://registry.npmjs.org/@fal-ai/client/-/client-1.11.0.tgz), [ComfyUI nodes_hunyuan3d.py](https://github.com/comfyanonymous/ComfyUI/blob/master/comfy_api_nodes/nodes_hunyuan3d.py)
- **[READ]** TRELLIS.2: its export step decimates (example target 1,000,000; fal default 500,000), remeshes, UV-unwraps and bakes a texture of up to 4096 pixels; the GLB is exported opaque and the alpha channel must be connected by hand; no de-lighting option is documented — [microsoft/TRELLIS.2](https://github.com/microsoft/TRELLIS.2), [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://registry.npmjs.org/@fal-ai/client/-/client-1.11.0.tgz)
- **[READ]** SF3D and SPAR3D de-light the colour and predict material parameters by design, and can quad-remesh with Instant Meshes — [stable-fast-3d](https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-fast-3d)
- **[READ]** Hitem3D exposes a `shading` "De-shading strength" (default 0.5) — [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://registry.npmjs.org/@fal-ai/client/-/client-1.11.0.tgz)
- **[READ]** Learned "artist-style" retopology is far below character budgets: MeshAnything "cannot generate meshes with more than 800 faces" (V2: 1,600) — [MeshAnything](https://github.com/buaacyw/MeshAnything). (2024.)

**Recommended practice**
- **[SECONDARY]** A Blender pipeline guide recommends using the AI mesh as a Shrinkwrap target, drawing new topology over it (Poly Build or RetopoFlow), then baking with "the AI mesh as the high-poly source, your retopologized mesh as the target", using tangent-space normals — [StraySpark pipeline guide](https://strayspark.studio/blog/blender-to-game-engine-pipeline-2026)
- **[SECONDARY]** Raw outputs are described as "dense geometry with poor edge flow", with hand retopology taking 8 to 16 hours for an experienced artist (a vendor blog's figure) — [Neural4D pipeline guide](https://blog.neural4d.com/industrial/3d-animation-pipeline/)
- **[SECONDARY]** Advice to judge assets only "after retopology, UVs, PBR review, rigging, collision, LODs and engine tests", and the cost argument that "a cheap model that needs two hours of retopology can cost more than a premium generation that needs twenty minutes" — [wavect.io guide](https://wavect.io/blog/ai-3d-model-generators-game-development-2026/)
- **[SECONDARY]** A Tripo-published workflow post treats AI models as "exceptional starting points, not final assets" and tests them by posing to extreme configurations to reveal mesh deformation and texture stretching; another says hero characters for skeletal animation "require retopology before import" — [Tripo blog: human-in-the-loop editing](https://www.tripo3d.ai/blog/explore/human-in-the-loop-editing-for-production-readiness)
- **[EXTRACT]** A 2026 arXiv paper frames retopology, UV layout, material authoring and rigging after generation as a distinct "assetization" stage (abstract only seen) — [arXiv 2604.23629](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.23629v2)
- **[READ]** For web delivery, fal's TRELLIS.2 schema advises a decimation target of "20k-50k for web/mobile" against a default of 500,000 — [@fal-ai/client 1.11.0](https://registry.npmjs.org/@fal-ai/client/-/client-1.11.0.tgz)

### Inferences
- De-lighting is uneven and should be checked per model: on by default in Tripo's v3.5 texture model, a flag on Rodin, a strength on Hitem3D, built in to Stability's models, available only for the older texture model at Meshy, and absent from TRELLIS.2's documented options. Baked highlights and shadows in the colour map are therefore still a live risk with Meshy 7 and TRELLIS.2 textures.
- UV unwrap services cap input at 30,000–80,000 faces (Hunyuan <30,000; Meshy ≤40,000; Tripo ≤80,000 triangles), so the order of operations is forced: reduce or retopologise first, unwrap second, then re-bake or re-texture (for example with Tripo's texture model or `fal-ai/trellis-2/retexture`).
- For a face seen close, the sensible use of a generator is as the sculpt: bake its detail to normal and colour maps on a mesh with known, symmetric, animation-ready topology. The generators' own quad modes produce quad-dominant surfaces but nothing read claims facial edge loops.

### Gaps
- No source on wrapping a production head topology (for example a MetaHuman or Character Creator base, or a commercial wrap tool) onto generated scans; the one search on it returned nothing specific.
- No measured quality of the vendors' own quad or low-poly passes on faces.
- No documentation found of three.js-specific guidance from any vendor.
