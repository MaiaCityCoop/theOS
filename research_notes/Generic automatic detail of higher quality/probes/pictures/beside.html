Each row is one size on screen: the size at which one of today's levels is first drawn. Every picture is drawn at exactly that many pixels and then shown larger, so its pixels can be seen.

Shape and cover only, seen from the side: one green, a card drawn as 45 % sprig, no painting, no light, no wind. "Outline" and "a neighbourhood off" are measured from thirteen sides against the full plant: how much of the picture the plant touches, and how much a small patch (four pixels by four; of a plant under 32 pixels an eighth of it) differs on average in what it shows of leaf and of wood. Drawn by a plain WebGL page for this research, not by the game.
