Today: one plant at each of its levels

The same plant, the same scale, seen from the side, as each of its levels draws it. "Outline": how much of the picture the crown touches, beside its nearest level. A crown that is the same at every level would read 100 % everywhere.

Shape and cover only: one green, a card drawn as 45 % sprig, no painting, no light, no wind. Between two levels the game draws both and lets sprigs shrink and grow one after another; this shows what it moves between. Drawn by a plain WebGL page for this research, not by the game.
