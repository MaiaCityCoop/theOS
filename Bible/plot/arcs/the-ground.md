# The Ground

Track 4, the city itself. Opens at step 29, 832,040 paying citizens, with the land and the sovereignty, and runs to 2042. The jump it asks for is everything. See [the ladder](../../worldbuilding/systems/the-ladder.md).

[Maia City](../../worldbuilding/locations/maia-city.md): a sovereign solarpunk city state for 1.3 million people, built from the ground up. This is the arc the hard stake belongs to. In 2042 it stands, or it does not.

## Setup

The primitives are proven at the scale of a thousand. Nothing about them is proven at the scale of a million, and the city needs something the first three tracks never did: land, and the right to govern it.

## Rising Action

1. The site and the sovereignty arrangement, which is the hardest external problem in the whole project.
2. Ground is broken, and for the first time the thing being built is the thing itself.
3. The underground goes in first, because nothing else can be placed until logistics exist.
4. The domes multiply at factory rate, which is what makes 16 years possible at all.
5. Maia takes the mayoralty: 1.3 million people's intent to execute instead of one man's.
6. Population crosses from settlers to citizens, and Samuel's share becomes one share among millions.

## Climax

2042. One point three million people living in a city they own, or a number short of it. There is no partial credit written into the stake, which is what makes it a stake.

## Resolution

The city stands as the blueprint, and the twin ships it to whoever wants to build the next one. Samuel is a citizen with his own stack and no more, in a city that no longer needs him to believe in it.
