# The First World

Track 1, the game. Opens at step 1, the first citizen, and runs to 2042. The jump it asks for is walking in, then an evening.

**Phase one of [the ladder](../../worldbuilding/systems/the-ladder.md) belongs entirely to this arc**: steps 1 to 12, about the first 147 days, in which twelve dome scales are built in the twin and nothing physical is promised. On step 13, day 165, the 144 m district dome completes the ladder and the first real dome leaves the game on the same day, handing over to [A Night in the Future](a-night-in-the-future.md).

The city in [the twin](../../worldbuilding/locations/the-twin.md) grows from a world you can walk around into a full digital twin of the real city: the blueprint for AI-native sovereign city states, and for the cities that come after this one.

## Setup

The ground is empty and the vision lives in one man's head. Nobody can be convinced by a description, because people only believe what they can see moving. So the first thing built is not a dome. It is a world you can stand inside tonight.

## Rising Action

1. The world opens and people can walk around the city before any of it exists.
2. [Maia](../../characters/maia.md) starts executing player intent, not just Samuel's, and the city begins taking a shape he did not draw.
3. The twin becomes accurate enough that what stands in it is a real proposal for what gets poured.
4. The best players stop playing and start wanting the real thing, which feeds [The Thousand](the-thousand.md).
5. The twin becomes a true digital twin, mirroring the built city, and other cities start copying it.

## Climax

The day a build in the twin becomes the drawing the real city is built from, with no translation step. The game stops being a representation and becomes the design.

## Resolution

The twin outlives its purpose as a recruiting tool and becomes infrastructure: the blueprint any city can take, which is how one city turns into a template for everyone.
