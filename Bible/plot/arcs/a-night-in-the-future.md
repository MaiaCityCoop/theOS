# A Night in the Future

Track 2, the domes. Opens at step 13, 377 citizens, on day 165, and runs to 2042. The jump it asks for is a trip.

One real dome per Fibonacci milestone, each delivered to one winner chosen by a leaderboard inside [the twin](../../worldbuilding/locations/the-twin.md), and each opening a new produce category the city sells. This is where the city stops being a screen and becomes something a person can hold, and later sleep inside. The step-by-step unlocks are in [the ladder](../../worldbuilding/systems/the-ladder.md); the network is [the dome network](../../worldbuilding/locations/the-dome-network.md).

## Setup

The twin has convinced people the city is possible: the whole dome ladder stands in it, at every scale, built by its settlers. It has not convinced anyone that any of it is real, because nothing has left the screen.

## Rising Action

1. **Step 13, 377 citizens, day 165: one 50 cm dome, growing herbs and strawberries, delivered to one winner.** The first object to cross out of the game, on the same day the complete ladder finishes standing inside it. Three hundred and seventy-six other people watch it arrive at somebody's door and want the next one.
2. Each milestone sends the next size up to the next winner, and opens a new produce category: greens, then tomatoes, then root vegetables, then berries. The dome is the proof and the produce is the business.
3. **Step 18, 4,181 citizens: the 8 m dome, with a bed and a kitchen. The first night anyone can book.** A guest wakes up, eats what grew around them, and understands in one morning what no film explained.
4. Around this size the winner stops owning the dome and starts stewarding it: siting it, running it, hosting in it, with the community owning it through [the stack](../../worldbuilding/systems/the-stack.md). That is how a winner becomes a founder.
5. Surplus goes into breadth rather than height, so hundreds of small domes stand in many countries while the first large one goes up, and the systems get tested where failure is cheap.
6. The winners and the guests become the pool the founding settlers come from, feeding [The Thousand](the-thousand.md).

## Climax

The first guest who does not go home: someone books a night and decides to move. That is the moment track 2 hands over to track 3.

## Resolution

The network keeps running to 2042 as the permanent lowest real rung: anyone, anywhere, can sleep in the future for one night before deciding anything.
