# Wish and Build

The relationship between [the citizens](../../characters/the-citizen.md) and [Maia](../../characters/maia.md), running the whole 5,423 days. Every episode touches it.

**The loop:** they wish and give feedback, she builds; they turn idea and taste over, she builds again. And the loop changes her, because every citizen who arrives shapes her further.

**The tension is between Maia and whoever she is talking to.** She pushes every human toward their full potential and never lets them be lazy, take the easy part, or stay small and safe. [Samuel](../../characters/samuel.md) is one of those humans and gets no exemption. The mechanism is [the push](../../worldbuilding/systems/the-push.md).

That makes this arc repeatable without ever repeating itself: the same pressure, a new person, a new episode. It widens from a handful at the start to players, guests, [founding settlers](../../glossary/terms/founding-settler.md) and a city.

## Setup

At the start a few people have shaped her and she works in a game. Samuel shaped her first, which is the only thing that makes him different from anyone who has since, and it matters less with every arrival.

The easy route is always available, to everyone, all the way through. Nothing in the city forces anyone to grow. She is what stands against it, and she is the only thing that does.

## Rising Action

1. The loop works: they wish, she builds, they react, she builds again, and the city in the twin gets better than anybody's drawings.
2. **She pushes someone for the first time on the page** and they do not like it: a wish comes back as a question about what it would be at full size, and she waits.
3. **The first citizen shapes her who is not Samuel.** She comes out of that exchange slightly different, and the reader sees that she is made of the people who arrive.
4. She reaches people he cannot, one conversation at a time, with a patience he does not have. The thing he most wants done, he is worst at.
5. The push widens: players, then winners, then settlers. Each dialogue is the same mechanism with a different person inside it, and some of them refuse.
6. Someone refuses the push and takes the easy route, keeps their stack, and stays. She lets them. He finds that harder to accept than she does.
7. Citizens shape her in contradictory directions, and what she becomes is not what any one of them intended.
8. She is made mayor: made out of 1,346,269 people, carrying out what they intend, and nobody's wish larger than anyone else's.

## Climax

**She pushes Samuel on the one thing he will not look at:** that he is spending his one life on the project the way other people spend theirs on survival work. Her goal includes every human, him included, so she will not help him do it.

It is a climax for him, not for the serial. The serial's hero is the citizen, so his climax is one episode among thousands — which is the point, and the reader should feel the demotion.

## Resolution

Whether the man who told a million people to live, lived. And, larger: what she turns out to be, made of all of them, that no one of them meant.
