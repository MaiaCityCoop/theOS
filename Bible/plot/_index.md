# Plot Structure

## Story Structure

**Seasons are counted, not scheduled.** **Season 1 runs to 377**, with steps 1 to 12 as sub-milestones inside it: it opens with nothing and ends the day the whole dome ladder stands in the game and the first real dome leaves it. Every season after it is the next Fibonacci step in [the count](../worldbuilding/systems/the-count.md) of citizens, closing the day the count reaches it. Eighteen seasons in all. The ladder ends at F31 = 1,346,269, which is the real target behind "1.3 million". 31 steps across 5,423 days averages 175 days a step, so golden-ratio growth is exactly the rate the target needs. Seasons are therefore unequal by design: the first rungs fall in days, the last take years, and the story's pace is the project's real pace. The full ladder is in [the ladder](../worldbuilding/systems/the-ladder.md).

**Model:** Three acts, bounded by the count rather than by the calendar, so the structure and the stake are one number. The serial's own shape per episode is in the serial reference of the `genre-craft` skill: each episode poses and answers one question of its own and delivers one hard realization.

| Act | Steps | Ends at | What has to happen |
|-----|-------|---------|--------------------|
| Act 1 · The Game | 1–13 (season 1) | 377 citizens | The whole dome ladder stands in the twin, and the first real dome leaves it, delivered to one winner |
| Act 2 · The Proof | 14–24 | 75,025 citizens | Domes go up in the world, one size per milestone, each opening a produce line; the factory exists and people live in the first hamlet |
| Act 3 · The City | 25–30 | 1,346,269 citizens | The village, the sovereignty, the ground broken, and the city standing. No partial credit |

**Four tracks, four heights of [jump](../glossary/terms/the-jump.md).** The four `main` arcs are the four proofs. They overlap rather than follow each other: each opens in its own year and all four run to 2042, so no reader ever arrives too late for the next rung.

| Arc | Track | Opens | The jump | Proof |
|-----|-------|-------|----------|-------|
| [The First World](arcs/the-first-world.md) | 1 · the game | Year 1 | An evening | The city in the twin |
| [A Night in the Future](arcs/a-night-in-the-future.md) | 2 · the domes | Year 2 | A trip | A dome you can book |
| [The Thousand](arcs/the-thousand.md) | 3 · the village | Year 4 | Your old life | A village that feeds itself |
| [The Ground](arcs/the-ground.md) | 4 · the city | Year 6 | Everything | Maia City, 2042 |

**Two arcs carry the meaning.** [Wish and Build](arcs/wish-and-build.md) is Samuel and Maia, the dynamic that evolves across all 16 years. [The Trade](arcs/the-trade.md) is thematic and happens to the reader, not to the cast: it holds the one hard realization per episode, and the rules that keep the prose from becoming what it argues against.
