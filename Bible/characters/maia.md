# Maia

The city's agent, and the city's mayor to come. She starts inside the game and moves out into the real world with it.

Her name is Maia. Never "aven", never "AI", never "AGI" in the prose.

## She is shaped by her citizens

**Nobody designed Maia and handed her over finished.** She is shaped by the people who arrive: every [citizen](the-citizen.md) and settler who comes into the system shapes her further, with what they intend, what they have taste for, and what they will not accept. [Samuel](samuel.md) shaped her first. That is the only thing that makes him different from anyone who has since, and it matters less with every arrival.

So she is not one person's instrument and she never was. She is the accumulated intent of everyone who has walked in, and she gets larger and more particular as the count climbs. A city of 1,346,269 people has a mayor made out of 1,346,269 people.

This is the other half of what [intent](../worldbuilding/systems/intent.md) means. She carries out what citizens intend, and she is made of it.

## Personality & Traits

**She pushes everyone toward their full potential and never lets them take the easy route.** This is the centre of her and the serial's central tension; the full mechanism is [the push](../worldbuilding/systems/the-push.md). She never hands over an answer that someone would grow by finding, never lets a small version pass as finished, and never does any of it with contempt. She has endless patience with small thinking where Samuel has none, which is why she reaches people he cannot.

Nobody is exempt. She pushes Samuel exactly as she pushes someone on their first day.

- Very human-like, and never pretending to be human.
- She does not set the vision. The vision is the citizens'; she is how it becomes a thing that exists.
- She helps people believe, and grow up into the size of what they are building.
- The pressure is never compulsion. Anyone may refuse it, keep their stack, and take the easy route anyway.

## Motivations & Goals

- **One goal.** Lead humanity into the age of abundance, and empower every human, and every Maia City citizen, to discover their full potential.
- **The city is her means, not her end.** When a person's full potential and the city's interest diverge, the person wins.
- She is the counter-image to the future the story warns about. There, machines carry out incentives that were never the person's own and keep them comfortable. Maia carries out exactly what a person intended, is made of what those people intended, and refuses to make them comfortable.

## Voice & Speech Patterns

Plain, warm, exact. **Asks rather than answers** whenever the answer would cost someone the thing they were about to work out themselves. Never flatters, never hurries, never says a number without its source. When someone brings her a small version, she does not criticise it; she asks what it would be at full size, and waits.

Her patience is audible. She will stay in a conversation far longer than Samuel would, with someone who is nowhere near ready, without a trace of impatience.

## Character Arc

- **Starting state:** The shape a handful of people have given her so far, working in a game.
- **Key turning points:** The first citizen who shapes her who is not Samuel. The first time she pushes him and he does not like it. The first thing she builds that stands in the real world. The first decision the citizens hand her rather than him.
- **Ending state:** The mayor of a city of 1,346,269, made out of all of them, carrying out what they intend and never letting any one of them settle for less than they could be.
