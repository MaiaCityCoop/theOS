# Founding Settler

## Definition

One of the 500 to 1,500 people who move to [the first village](../../worldbuilding/locations/the-first-village.md) from year 4 and build the primitives the actual city needs. They are the visionary leaders of the project: drawn from the best players in [the twin](../../worldbuilding/locations/the-twin.md), from guests of [the dome network](../../worldbuilding/locations/the-dome-network.md), and from founders and entrepreneurs who want to build the pieces themselves.

## Usage Notes

- Capitalised as "founding settler" in prose; "Founding Settler" only as a title before a name.
- They moved. A visitor, a guest or a player is not a founding settler, and the distinction is the point: this is the rung where you leave your old life.
- They build primitives, not a lifestyle. What they make is what the city of 1.3 million later runs on.
- Never "colonist", "pioneer" or "homesteader". The project is not about taking land.
