# The Jump

## Definition

The act of leaving the safe life for the thing you actually want. Every track of the project asks for a jump of a different height, and underneath all of them sits the smallest one the project can ask for:

| Rung | What it costs |
|------|---------------|
| Walking in | One step into [the twin](../../worldbuilding/locations/the-twin.md), which makes you a citizen and an owner |
| An evening | Playing in [the twin](../../worldbuilding/locations/the-twin.md) |
| A trip | A night in [a dome](../../worldbuilding/locations/the-dome-network.md) |
| Your old life | Moving to [the first village](../../worldbuilding/locations/the-first-village.md) |
| Everything | [The city](../../worldbuilding/locations/maia-city.md) |

The ladder exists so that no reader is ever too far from the next rung. Nobody is asked to jump the whole distance at once, and the first rung is walking in.

## Usage Notes

- "Jump as high as you can" is the invitation. It is always an invitation, never an instruction or a reproach.
- A jump is aimed at a rung the reader can actually reach today. A call to jump with nothing to jump to is the thing this project refuses to do.
- The opposite of the jump is not cowardice; it is the trade made in the name of survival. Name the trade, never the person.
- Never call a reader an NPC, asleep, or dead. The realization is theirs to have, and the prose aims at what they were told their life was worth.
