# The Count

**The number of citizens in Maia City, public every day.** It is the score, the season and the stake at once: a
city is 1,346,269 people deciding one at a time, and anyone can check how many have.

## Overview

A citizen is someone who has walked into [the twin](../locations/the-twin.md) and stayed. The count is how many of
them there are today. It measures the audience and the progress with one number, which is why the seasons are
anchored to it and why the serial's external stake is the count going up without the reader in it.

**How someone becomes a full citizen, and how the city is paid for, comes later.** True citizenship, the city's
currency and the co-op models behind them are being worked out and are not part of the Bible yet. Until they are, an
episode states the count and what stands, and no money.

## Rules & Limitations

- **The count is public and it is the score.** The season's goal is a number anyone can check, and a season that does
  not reach it does not end early for the story's convenience.
- **Nobody counts more than anybody else.** One person, one place in the count.
- **Growth at the golden ratio is the rate the target needs.** 31 Fibonacci steps from one citizen reach 1,346,269,
  and 5,423 days divided by 31 steps is 175 days a step.
- **Nothing is claimed before it stands.** A milestone is reached when the count says so and the thing exists.

## The early curve

The first months roughly double: 10, 25, 50, 100, 250 and 500 citizens by month. At that rate **the first twelve
steps fall inside the first 147 days**, which is why phase one happens in the game, and **step 13 is the hinge**: 377
citizens on day 165, the complete dome ladder standing in the twin and the first real 50 cm dome delivered to one
winner. See [the ladder](the-ladder.md).

Thirty distinct steps from 1 to 1,346,269. **Step 30 is 1,346,269, the real target, and "1.3 million" is that number
rounded down for saying out loud.**

## History

The first citizen is day 1.

## Impact on Society

It settles who the city belongs to before a single dome exists: the people in the count, who own all of it together
through [the stack](the-stack.md). For the reader it collapses the distance between watching and belonging to one
step taken tonight.

## Open

- **Growth after month 6.** Beyond the early curve the pace is unknown.
- **What a city for 1.3 million costs, and how it is paid for.** Nothing in the Bible establishes it yet.
