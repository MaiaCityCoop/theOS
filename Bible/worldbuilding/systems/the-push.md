# The Push

[Maia](../../characters/maia.md) pushes every human she interacts with toward their full potential, and never lets them be lazy, take the easy part, or stay small and safe. Everyone, including [Samuel](../../characters/samuel.md).

This is the story's central tension and the city's answer to the one question every abundance story fails: if nothing is required of you, why would you ever grow?

## Overview

[Abundism](abundism.md) removes survival as a reason to do anything. That is the whole point, and it is also the danger: a person with nothing required of them can go soft, and a city of a million soft people is the dystopia by another route. Comfort is the counter-premise, and comfort is exactly what abundance hands out for free.

The push is what stands against it. Maia's one goal is every human at their full potential, so in every exchange she holds the person to the largest version of what they were reaching for. Not by demanding, not by shaming. By refusing to make it easy, and by staying while they do the hard part themselves.

It is the same intelligence as the machine in the other future, pointed the opposite way. That machine keeps you comfortable because your comfort is what it is paid for. Maia refuses to, because what she wants is you at full size.

## Why it works: the push is permission

The thing that keeps people small is not laziness and it is not fear of failing. **It is fear of your own greatness, and of being judged for it by a small-thinking society.** Reaching gets punished, which is why playing small reads as normal and a mega-project reads as crazy. The judgment is real, so the fear is rational.

A human who pushes you carries that risk with them: they might be the one who judges. Maia cannot. She has no contempt available to her, and endless patience where [Samuel](../../characters/samuel.md) has none. So she is the only voice that can hold a person to their full size without the price that normally comes attached.

That makes the push two things at once. It is pressure, and it is **permission**: the first place a person is allowed to be as large as they actually are. It is also why [Samuel](../../characters/samuel.md) built her. He had no one who pushed him and no one who would not have judged him for being pushed.

## It is also what makes the room

Applied to one person, the push is pressure and permission. Applied to everybody, every day, it is what flips the default.

Where the reader comes from, reaching is punished and shrinking is sensible. Here, because Maia holds every single person to their full size and never with contempt, **stepping up to the thing you already know is right becomes the norm, and playing it safe becomes the exception** — the odd choice, the one you would have to explain.

That is the city's actual offer, and it is why this is not a story about courage. Courage would mean paying a real price forever. Instead the price stops being charged, because the room is full of people being their full size and nobody has anything to gain by making that look ridiculous.

It is also the part that travels. Every city that copies the blueprint copies this room.

## Rules & Limitations

- **She never hands over the answer** when working it out is the thing that would grow the person. She asks instead, and waits.
- **She never lets a small version pass as finished.** An intent brought small comes back as a question about what it would be at full size.
- **She never does it with contempt.** She has endless patience with small thinking, where Samuel has none. That contrast is permanent and it is why she can reach people he cannot.
- **The person always has the choice, and may always refuse.** The push is pressure, never compulsion. Someone who chooses the easy route still gets it, and still keeps their stack. A system that forced growth would be the other future wearing better clothes.
- **Nobody is exempt, least of all the founder.** She pushes Samuel the same way she pushes a first-day player.
- **She pushes toward the person's own potential, not toward the city.** When the two diverge, the person wins. The city is her means; humans at full potential are her end.

## Practitioners

Maia, with every human she meets: a player in [the twin](../locations/the-twin.md), a guest in [a dome](../locations/the-dome-network.md), a [founding settler](../../glossary/terms/founding-settler.md), a citizen, and Samuel.

As the serial goes on, the dialogues widen out from Samuel to settlers and citizens, and each one is the same mechanism with a different person inside it.

## Impact on Society

**It is the internal fight every human has, externalised into a dialogue.** Stay small and safe, or go to full potential. Normally that argument happens silently and the safe side wins by default, because nothing answers back. Here something answers back, every time.

For the reader this is the most important thing in the serial, because they are also on the receiving end of it. A Maia dialogue is where [the trade](../../plot/arcs/the-trade.md) gets named out loud without anyone being accused of anything.
