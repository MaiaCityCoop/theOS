# The Twin

The city in the game. Track 1, open from the first citizen and running the whole 16 years, growing from a world you can walk around into a full digital twin of the real city: the blueprint for AI-native sovereign city states, and for the cities that come after this one.

**For the first 165 days it is the only thing that exists.** Phase one of [the ladder](../systems/the-ladder.md), steps 1 to 12, builds twelve dome scales in here, from the 5 cm desk dome to the 89 m dome, and promises nothing physical until the money is real. On step 13 the 144 m district dome completes the ladder and the first real dome leaves the game on the same day.

After that the twin is the audition: a leaderboard inside it chooses who receives each real dome, all the way up to a place among the founding settlers.

## Description

The same city as [Maia City](maia-city.md), on a different substrate. Everything a player builds here is a proposal for the thing that gets built in the ground, which is why the game is not a game about a city but the city's first draft.

## Culture & Customs

The twin is where a watcher becomes a player and a player becomes a founder. The jump it asks for is an evening, which is why it is the lowest rung on the ladder and the one everybody can reach tonight.

The best players and entrepreneurs here are the people who go on to build the real villages in track 3. Standing in the twin is the audition for the ground.

## Notable Features

- Playable from year 1, so the proof exists before anything is poured.
- Becomes a true digital twin: what stands here mirrors what stands there.
- The blueprint other cities copy, which is how one city becomes a template for everyone.
- Maia is in here from the start, executing what players intend.

## Current State

The first and, at the open, the only proof. Whatever stands here is the entire visible argument that the city is possible.

## Open

- Year one's finish line: what has to stand in the twin, or how many people have to be playing, for proof one to count as delivered.
