# The Dome Network

Real domes, spread around the world, owned by the community together. Track 2, which opens at step 13 of [the ladder](../systems/the-ladder.md) (377 paying citizens, day 165) and runs to 2042.

**One dome per Fibonacci milestone, each delivered to one winner** chosen by a leaderboard inside [the twin](the-twin.md), and each opening a new produce category the city sells. The first is a 50 cm dome growing herbs and strawberries, on the same day the complete ladder finishes standing in the game. From step 18 the 8 m dome has a bed and a kitchen, and that is the first night anyone can book.

## Description

One dome at a time, in real places, each one a working piece of the city rather than a show home: the geodesic greenhouse shell, the food growing in it, the systems that make it self-sufficient. A guest spends a night in the way of living the city is for, which is a stronger argument than any film of it.

Each dome grows. A single dome becomes a cluster, a cluster becomes a small village, and that is how track 2 turns into [the first village](the-first-village.md).

## Culture & Customs

The jump this track asks for is a trip. It is the second rung: too large for an evening, far smaller than moving your life. The guests who come here are the pool the founding settlers are drawn from.

## Notable Features

- Bookable for a night, anywhere in the world.
- Each dome is owned through the co-ops, like everything else in the project.
- Each one is a real test of the dome systems before the city depends on them.

Not built at the open. Opens in year 2.
