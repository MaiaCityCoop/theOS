# The First Village

The first real village, in the Mediterranean, built by 500 to 1,500 founding settlers. Track 3, which opens at step 25 of [the ladder](../systems/the-ladder.md) with the first hamlet and reaches 500 settlers at step 27. These are the visionary leaders who build all the primitives the actual city needs.

The name is a working one; the real village has not been named yet.

## Description

Domes grown together into a place people live in permanently: homes, food forests, the first factories, the first underground run, the first co-op governance that has to settle real disputes between real neighbours. Everything the city will need at the scale of 1.3 million is invented here at the scale of a thousand, where a mistake is survivable.

## The trajectory: trucked in, then self-sufficient, then export-positive

**At the start everything is trucked in**, and the serial must never pretend otherwise. A settlement on open ground eats what arrives by lorry.

The whole arc of a village, in the game and in the ground, is to work itself off those deliveries one at a time: plant the food forest, grow what it eats, make its own power and materials, reach **self-sufficiency** — and then go past it, until it **exports more than it brings in**. Sovereignty is the end of that road, not the beginning of it.

Saying "nothing is trucked in" is only true of the finished city in 2042. Of a new village it is a lie, and it throws away the most interesting thing the game has: the climb.

## Culture & Customs

The jump this track asks for is your old life. A founding settler does not visit; they move. They are drawn from the best players in [the twin](the-twin.md) and the guests of [the dome network](the-dome-network.md), plus founders and entrepreneurs who want to build the primitives themselves.

## Notable Features

- 500 to 1,500 people, which is small enough to govern face to face and large enough to need real systems.
- Mediterranean, so the climate suits year-round growing from the start.
- Every primitive the city depends on gets its first real test here.

## Current State

Not built at the open. The first hamlet comes at step 25 (121,393 citizens), the village site at step 26, 500 settlers at step 27, and 1,500 with a measured self-sufficient year at step 28.
