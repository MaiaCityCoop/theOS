# Five Centimetres

Step 1 unlocks a dome five centimetres across, in a game, against a city for 1,346,269 people. That is not a joke at the project's expense, it is the design: thirty steps, each a Fibonacci number of citizens, each unlocking the next dome size, the whole ladder built in the twin first and then again in the earth. Nobody is asked to believe in the city, only to look at the next rung.

Maia is small today too, because she is made of what people intend and almost nobody has arrived. Come in now and you are not joining something; you are one of the people it gets made out of.

Then the rung, once: a euro a day, the first piece of what you own, and the reason the count and the day's revenue are the same number.

Closes on the serial's whole stake, made immediate: tomorrow the number is either still one, or it is not.

**Notes.** `outcome: no-and` is the reader's: nothing is resolved and the count is now their problem too. The closing beat refuses flattery — they already knew whether this was for them, and knowing is the entry condition.
