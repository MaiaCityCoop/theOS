# Waking Inside Something You Own

Morning. You wake in a dome you own a share of, pick strawberries three metres from where you slept, and step outside into a city of 1.3 million people that sounds like a forest, because nothing moves on the surface. You check your stack the way people used to check a balance, and there is no job anywhere in it.

The strawberries carry the receipt: herbs and strawberries were the first produce category, unlocked at step 13 with 377 citizens and a 50 cm dome won by one person.

**Notes.** The quietness is the strongest claim in the project and the one a visitor feels in a second, so it gets the most sensory weight. No explanation of the underground beyond what a person would notice.
