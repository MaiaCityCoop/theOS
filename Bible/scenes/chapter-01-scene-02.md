# What It Would Be at Full Size

Midday. You bring Maia an idea you think is good. She does not say it is good. She asks what it would look like if it were the whole of what it could be, and then she waits, without an edge in her voice, for as long as it takes. You make it bigger, and she was right that you had stopped early.

Seven people and Maia are taking on what would once have needed a government department and four hundred staff, and they are doing it because it is the most interesting thing available that morning.

**Notes.** This is the reader's first meeting with the push, delivered as a conversation rather than an explanation. Nobody is in a hammock: the ceiling came off, so teams got smaller and problems got enormous. `outcome: yes-but` — you get the idea through, but only after she makes you double it.
