# Pensions written into law, and Ida May Fuller

## Question

When did old-age pensions become law in Germany and the United States, and who received the first U.S. Social
Security check?

## Findings

- Germany: the Reichstag passed the invalidity and old-age insurance laws on 24 May 1889, with an old-age pension
  from 70. Source: Deutschland Museum, above.
- United States: the Social Security Act was signed on 14 August 1935, old-age benefits from 65, paid for by payroll
  contributions. Source: SSA history, above.
- Ida May Fuller of Vermont received check number 00-000-001, dated 31 January 1940, for $22.54. She paid $24.75 in
  contributions, collected $22,888.92 in benefits, and died in January 1975, aged 100. Source: SSA, above.
