# A dated forecast that slipped

## Question

Was one million robotaxis promised for 2020, and did it happen?

## Findings

- At Tesla's Autonomy Day, April 2019, one million robotaxis on the road in 2020 was promised. Source: Engadget,
  22 April 2019.
- By the end of 2020 there were none in service. Source: The Drive, above.
