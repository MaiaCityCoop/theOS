# Living Energy Farm and energy designed from the ground up

## Question

Does Living Energy Farm (Louisa County, Virginia) live a comfortable life on about 5% of the average American's
energy, by redesigning rather than bolting solar onto an ordinary house: thermal mass, sun-driven machines by day,
no large batteries, a thermal-mass fridge, a DC micro-grid, very little firewood?

## Findings

- It is an off-grid community on 127 acres in Louisa County, Virginia, run on a "direct drive" DC micro-grid:
  appliances and motors wired straight to the panels by day, with thermal storage and insulation instead of large
  batteries. Source: LEF's own DC micro-grid page and Microgrid Knowledge, above.
- Its own figures: the design needs about 8% as many panels and 2% as much battery capacity as conventional AC solar
  and storage for the same home services. Source: LEF, above.
- The "5% of the average American's energy" figure: not found in LEF's material. The 72-hour fridge and the two
  buckets of wood a year: not checked yet.
