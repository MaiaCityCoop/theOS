# Le Passage and capital-independent ownership

## Question

Is there a French community called Le Passage whose members have one vote each whatever capital they put in, where
speculation is blocked, a member can leave with their money, there is no individual property but private spaces,
members are chosen together, and the project outlives every member?

## Findings

- The video describes Le Passage this way, and says later members are meant to join free once it is paid off.
  Source: the video above.
- A web search (2026-11-17) found no project of that name with these rules.
- The principle is real elsewhere: La Traverse, a French housing co-operative, takes decisions on "one inhabitant,
  one vote", disconnected from the value of the flat each occupies, by consent (sociocracy). Source: the New
  European Bauhaus entry above.
- French "coopératives d'habitants" are the legal family these rules come from. Source: Reporterre, above.

## Story Use

Not used. The principle feeds the stack (one citizen one vote, an exit, no speculation) as the city's own design.
Le Passage is not named on screen until a primary source (its statutes or its own site) is found. The legal form
needs a qualified reviewer before any episode states it.
