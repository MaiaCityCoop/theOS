1.3 million humans build their own solarpunk city.

Welcome to Day – of 5,423.

Why

Imagine we could start clean.

No old city to fix, no old rules to work around. A meadow, a river, and every choice still open.

Work you choose

Machines carry the work nobody wants. People spend their days on what they love, and on each other.

Wealth that stays

What the city makes belongs to the people who build it. Every citizen owns a share of the whole.

A forest you can eat

Food grows where we live: fruit, nuts and greens one step from every door, a forest that feeds us.

A hundred times better way to live. First in game, then in real.
