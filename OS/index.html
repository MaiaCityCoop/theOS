Welcome to Day – of 5,423.

2042

Wake up in the city you own.

Light through a dome that is also a greenhouse. Breakfast three steps from where you slept, still on the plant. Outside, a city of 1.3 million that sounds like a forest, because everything that moves runs beneath it. What you pay each month goes into co-ops you hold a share of, so it comes back as the roof over your head, the food in the ground and the factory that made them both. Nobody here is working to stay alive. What people do instead is the largest thing they are each capable of, in small teams, on the problems worth cracking.

A forest you can eat

Food grows where we live: fruit, nuts and greens one step from every door, a forest that feeds us.

Wealth that stays

What the city makes belongs to the people who build it. Every citizen owns a share of the whole.

Your full potential

Nobody sells their hours to stay alive. What is left is the thing you would have chosen anyway — and nothing here lets you settle for the small version of it.

The game

Not another city builder. A civilisation-scale brick builder for a new generation of sovereign cities.

Think of maiaCITY as a research laboratory you can play. Every village built in it tests one of the building blocks a new civilisation needs: how we grow our food, share our energy, own what we build and decide together. We try them in the game, long before a single real brick is laid.

Manifesto

We were never built to survive. We were built to thrive.

IThe old blueprint

For generations we have been handed a blueprint drawn for scarcity. Get through the month. Compete for what is left. Rent our own lives back, one invoice at a time.

That blueprint is running out. Not in fire — in the quiet way a thing ends once nobody believes in it any more.

And the next one will not draw itself.

We are far more capable than we have ever been allowed to believe.

IIThe vision

  • We see a million founders uniting behind one vision, and discovering what humans have always been able to do the moment they stop competing for scraps.
  • We see a child growing up who never once hears that this is just the way things are.
  • We see the elders of a village teaching what they know to the people who will outlive them, and being needed again.
  • We see a place built for humans to flourish in. A place where what we build together, we own together, and abundance is no rumour from somewhere else, just what people working in flow naturally produce.
  • We see work that feels like purpose, and days that feel like life.

IIIBuild it

  1. So build it in the fields, where the soil comes back.
  2. Build it in the workshops, where the machines are ours to repair.
  3. Build it in the kitchens, where fifty people sit down to eat together.
  4. Build it in every person who has felt, for years, that there must be more than this.

Not 100x more stuff. 100x more life.

And on the day a million founders stand inside what they built, they will know what we know deep in our hearts for sure:

when we unite in vision, the impossible becomes possible.

Samuel Andert

How it goes

  1. Sign up as a traveler. An e-mail address, nothing else. The world opens straight away and costs nothing.
  2. Walk around and find your village. Every village is being built by real people. Look at what they have made and how they live, then apply to the one you want to belong to.
  3. The village decides. Citizenship is theirs to give, not ours. When they vote you in, a notification arrives: your citizenship is waiting for you.
  4. Take up your citizenship. One euro a month. You get your character, your tent and your share, and your village is waiting.

Your first days

It starts in a tent, on open ground.

Build the first settlement

You and your fellow settlers put up the first co-living domes — each one a greenhouse too, with a food forest growing inside it, so the place that shelters you also feeds you.

Stop needing the lorries

At the start everything is trucked in. Plant the food forest, grow what you eat, and work the village off its deliveries one at a time — then past self-sufficiency, until it exports more than it brings in. That is the whole game.

Decide together

It all starts in your own tent. What goes up next, which plants go in the ground, where the water runs: the village decides it, and you have a voice in it from your first day.

Invest in the co-ops

Hold a share of the things the village runs on, and of the challenges it takes on.
