# The Window

## Definition

The time while work is still a lever you can pull: while the hours you sell can still be turned into ownership of the machines that are about to produce everything.

Machines will produce abundance either way. The only open question is who owns them, and that is being settled this decade. The window is not a date on a calendar; it is the stretch of time in which an ordinary person can still buy in with ordinary work, and it narrows as ownership consolidates.

## Usage Notes

- **Never give it a date.** The serial carries no dated forecast about AI. A borrowed deadline owes receipts the project does not control, and one that passes unmet fails in public with thousands of episodes still to write. "This decade" and "while you still can" are true and never expire.
- Capitalised as "the window". Never "the Window".
- It narrows and it closes. It does not "run out" or "expire".
- Say what it is a window for: owning something, jumping, building. A window on its own is vague.
- It is never a threat of violence, collapse or dystopia on the page. It is a door narrowing quietly while people are busy being sensible.
- The serial's hard stake is the city itself, built by the people who walk in, not the window. The window says why walking in now matters; the city is what is actually built, in public, every day.
