# Plot Structure

## Story Structure

**Model:** Three acts, bounded by what has been built rather than by the calendar, so the story's pace is the project's real pace. The serial's own shape per episode is in the serial reference of the `genre-craft` skill: each episode poses and answers one question of its own and delivers one hard realization.

| Act | Tracks | Ends when | What has to happen |
|-----|--------|-----------|--------------------|
| Act 1 · The Game | 1 · the game | The first real dome leaves the game | Every dome size stands in the twin, and the first real dome leaves it, delivered to one winner |
| Act 2 · The Proof | 2 · the domes | People live in the first hamlet | Domes go up in the world, one size after another, each opening a produce line; the factory exists and people live in the first hamlet |
| Act 3 · The City | 3 · the village, 4 · the city | The city stands | The village, the sovereignty, the ground broken, and the city standing. No partial credit |

**Four tracks, four heights of [jump](../glossary/terms/the-jump.md).** The four `main` arcs are the four proofs. They open in order, as the people arrive, and overlap rather than follow each other: once open, each runs to 2042, so no reader ever arrives too late for the next step.

| Arc | Track | Opens | The jump | Proof |
|-----|-------|-------|----------|-------|
| [The First World](arcs/the-first-world.md) | 1 · the game | Year 1 | An evening | The city in the twin |
| [A Night in the Future](arcs/a-night-in-the-future.md) | 2 · the domes | Year 2 | A trip | A dome you can book |
| [The Thousand](arcs/the-thousand.md) | 3 · the village | Year 4 | Your old life | A village that feeds itself |
| [The Ground](arcs/the-ground.md) | 4 · the city | Year 6 | Everything | Maia City, 2042 |

**Two arcs carry the meaning.** [Wish and Build](arcs/wish-and-build.md) is Samuel and Maia, the dynamic that evolves across all 16 years. [The Trade](arcs/the-trade.md) is thematic and happens to the reader, not to the cast: it holds the one hard realization per episode, and the rules that keep the prose from becoming what it argues against.
