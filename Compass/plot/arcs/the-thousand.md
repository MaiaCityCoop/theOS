# The Thousand

Track 3, the first village. Opens third, as the people arrive, with the first hamlet, and runs to 2042. The jump it asks for is your old life.

Between 500 and 1,500 [founding settlers](../../glossary/terms/founding-settler.md) move to the Mediterranean and build every primitive the city will later run on. See [the first village](../../worldbuilding/locations/the-first-village.md).

## Setup

Thousands of people have played the city and hundreds have slept in a dome. None of them have left their life for it, because nothing yet asks them to and nowhere yet exists to go.

## Rising Action

1. The site is chosen and the first settlers commit, giving up what they had for a share of what is not built yet.
2. The domes become homes rather than rooms: permanent, year-round, feeding the people in them.
3. The first real test of [the stack](../../worldbuilding/systems/the-stack.md): ownership that has to settle disputes between neighbours who cannot leave.
4. The first run of [the underground](../../worldbuilding/systems/the-underground.md), at a scale where getting it wrong is survivable.
5. A primitive fails in a way the twin never predicted, and the village has to invent its way out.

## Climax

The village feeds, governs and powers itself for a full year with nothing coming in from outside. Self-sufficiency stops being a design claim and becomes a measured fact.

## Resolution

The primitives exist and have been proven at the scale of a thousand. Everything the city needs is now known to work, which is what lets [The Ground](the-ground.md) break.
