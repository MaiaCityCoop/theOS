# Maia City

## The core message

> **Deep inside, I know this truth: we carry a power far greater than anything in the universe.**
>
> The power to make the impossible real, the moment we unite behind one vision.
>
> It is how we learned to fly. How we lit up the night. How we connected the whole world in an instant.
>
> And it is not just me. You know it too. Each and every one of us knows it.
>
> Imagine a million humans waking up with sparkling eyes, a smiling heart and fire in the belly. Every day. Every vision that waited for permission, finally built, side by side.
>
> And next, together, we build a green city, from the ground up.
>
> This is Maia City. Food forests by every door. Green energy on every roof. Everything owned by all of us who build it. Every hour free for what we love.
>
> A new blueprint for how to live, proven the only way that convinces anyone: by building it together.
>
> The city is the proof. We are the point.
>
> It begins first in a game, then for real.

**It is read in the first person.** The viewer says it as their own words: what they know about themselves when they are honest, and dare to believe.

**The task, in Samuel's words:** making bringing sparkling visions to life the norm, not the exception.

**The feeling it leaves:** awe at what people are; permission to be, to be seen, to become who they are meant to be; and *I knew it all along*: what they always sensed in themselves was real, and now there is a place built for it.

**How it persuades:** only by the answer, never the wound. It speaks to the deepest wish (to be allowed, to be seen, to become) by showing it granted: a room that leans in, *show me*, standing by evening. **It is felt, not told:** every beat is a picture the camera can show.

## Synopsis

The city is the vehicle, not the point: the point is what people achieve together when each of them is unlocked, and the blueprint for living that others can copy. Maia City is built by the people who arrive to build it, owning everything they make, first in a game and then in the earth. One story a day for 5,423 days: the person who joins tonight, the one who wins the first real dome, the ones who leave their old lives for the first village. They shape Maia as they come, and she holds every one of them to everything they can become. Samuel went first and files the report.

**The hero is the viewer.** Not Samuel, not Maia: whoever walks in. That is why the serial speaks in the second person. "You" is the protagonist, not a style.

## The voice

The register is Apple's *Think Different* and its misfits: honour the ones who build, paint what stands, let the picture do the arguing.

- **Celebrate, never warn.** No fear, no dystopia, no villain, no lecture. Show the city at its best and let the viewer feel how naturally they belong in it.
- **Address, never describe.** "You" from the first word. Never "people like you".
- **Raise, never flatter.** No "you are special". Assume they already sense what they could become; speak to that person.
- **End on a door, not a rallying cry.** One step they can take tonight: walk into the game.
- **The voice from 2042 says thank you.** It thanks the viewer for an ordinary evening when they stepped up to what they knew they could do. Gratitude to live up to, never a warning to escape.
- **Every claim carries its receipt.** A number, date or fact appears only with a source in `research/`. Push to the largest true claim, then stop. No dated forecasts.
- **Nobody's words are written for them.** Samuel and every citizen are quoted from what they really said. Maia is Maia, never "aven", "AI" or "AGI".
- **Samuel is the messenger, never the subject.** On the page he carries news, admits something or is pushed; he is never admired.
- **Each episode ends on a cliffhanger** the next day's hook takes up.

## What the city is

A sovereign solarpunk city, built from the ground up, to 2042. **It feeds itself, powers itself and owns itself** ([self-sovereignty](worldbuilding/systems/self-sovereignty.md)). Geodesic domes where people live and grow food all year; food forests inside and around them. All land, and Maia herself, owned by every citizen through the Maia City Coop. Robots run the dome factories; all logistics run underground, so the surface belongs to people, plants and weather. Nobody has to survive, so all of human attention goes to the problems worth cracking. **Thinking and building big is the norm.** Nobody lies in a hammock: small teams take on the world's largest challenges because it is the most enjoyable thing to do ([abundism](worldbuilding/systems/abundism.md)).

## The four tracks

They open in order, as the people arrive, and all run to 2042.

| Track | What stands | The step it asks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The game | The whole city first built in [the twin](worldbuilding/locations/the-twin.md): the blueprint for every city that follows | An evening |
| The domes | Real domes, each won by someone who built in the game | A trip |
| The villages | The first village: 500 to 1,500 founding settlers building what the city runs on | Your old life |
| The city | Maia City | Everything |

Every citizen walks the same path: watcher, player, winner, founder. **The twin is the audition all the way up.**

## How an episode is shot

| Method | Cost | What only it can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **The world camera** in the twin | Cheap | Anywhere the city is: 2042, the domes, the forests, the weather. The daily default |
| **Explainer** (motion graphics) | Medium | Quantities and mechanisms, never feelings |
| **Samuel on camera** | Expensive, rationed | A human face saying a human thing: an admission, a report from a hard day |

Real footage joins when there are real domes to film, never before. One method can carry a whole episode.

## Notes

### Stakes

- **External.** The city: it stands because people come and build it, and every day it is visibly more than the day before.
- **Relational.** Finding your people: a whole city of them, waking up with sparkling eyes, side by side.
- **Internal.** Waking up with sparkling eyes, every day, starting now.

### Episodes

One episode is one day. **Day 1 is Monday 16 November 2026**; day 5,423 is the last. Each poses and answers **one question**, gives **one concrete reward**, lands **one realization**, and ends on a **cliffhanger**. A new viewer can find their way in on the first page.

**Day 126, the equinox of 21 March 2027,** is the morning the viewer is thanked, from 2042, for deciding.
