# The Twin Oaks membership door

## Question

How does Twin Oaks admit members: a three-week visitor program, a six-month provisional membership, then an 80% vote,
with later review?

## Findings

- The three-week visitor period is the first step and a prerequisite for membership; visitors join the work and
  social life and attend orientations. Source: Global Ecovillage Network and FIC listings, above.
- When the community is full, applicants may wait about three to nine months for a place. Source: FIC listing.
- A six-month provisional membership appears in an archived bylaw quoted in a cohousing policy example; not confirmed
  against Twin Oaks's current bylaws.
- The 80% vote: not found. Source to check: twinoaks.org, its bylaws.

## Story Use

Not used. It informs the city's own design (the gate and the door). If an episode names Twin Oaks, only the
confirmed visitor period is stated until the rest is found on twinoaks.org.
