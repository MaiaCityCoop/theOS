# Worldbuilding

## World Overview

This world is ours, now, moving forward. Nothing here is invented: it is the world as it is between 2026 and 2042, and the city is the thing being built in it.

**The fork.** Machines will produce abundance either way. The only open question is who owns them, and that is being settled this decade, while work is still a lever an ordinary person can pull — [the window](../glossary/terms/the-window.md). On one side of the fork, the machines carry out incentives that were never the person's own, and people are fed, entertained and owned. On the other, people own the machines and the machines carry out what people intend. Maia City is built to prove the second side is reachable, and to be the blueprint other cities copy.

The serial never puts a date on that fork. Its hard stake is the city instead: **people deciding one at a time, in public, to build it.**

**The city.** [Maia City](locations/maia-city.md) is a sovereign solarpunk city state, built from the ground up by 2042 by the people who live in it. It runs on [abundism](systems/abundism.md), owns itself through [the stack](systems/the-stack.md), governs and builds by [intent](systems/intent.md), lives and eats inside [the domes](systems/the-domes.md), and moves everything through [the underground](systems/the-underground.md).

**Its key pattern is [self-sovereignty](systems/self-sovereignty.md):** it feeds itself, powers itself and owns itself. Food from the food forests, energy from a city built for the sun, and ownership through the Maia City Coop, which owns all the land, so every citizen does, and Maia is owned by everyone too. A new village starts with everything trucked in and climbs to all three.

**Four proofs, four heights of [jump](../glossary/terms/the-jump.md).** The city becomes real in four overlapping tracks that open in order, as the people arrive, each one something a person can touch, each one asking more than the last. [The twin](locations/the-twin.md) from year 1 costs an evening. [The dome network](locations/the-dome-network.md) from year 2 costs a trip. [The first village](locations/the-first-village.md) from year 4 costs your old life. The city itself, from year 6, costs everything. All four run to 2042, so no reader ever arrives too late for the next step.

**Every citizen walks the same arc:** watcher, then player, then [founding settler](../glossary/terms/founding-settler.md).

**And it belongs to the people who build it.** The Maia City Coop owns all the land, so every citizen does, and Maia is owned by everyone too. That settles who the city belongs to before a dome exists. How it is paid for comes back later, with true citizenship.

**And something pushes them the whole way.** Abundism removes survival as a reason to do anything, which is its point and its danger: nothing required of you is also nothing making you grow. [The push](systems/the-push.md) is the city's answer. Maia holds every human she meets to their full potential and never lets them take the easy route, the founder included. It is the internal fight every person has, stay small or go full size, turned into a conversation that answers back.
