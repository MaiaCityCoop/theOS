# The First Village

The first real village, in the Mediterranean, built by 500 to 1,500 founding settlers. Track 3, which opens third, as the people arrive, with the first hamlet, and grows to 500 settlers and then 1,500. These are the visionary leaders who build all the primitives the actual city needs.

The name is a working one; the real village has not been named yet.

## Description

Domes grown together into a place people live in permanently: homes, food forests, the first factories, the first underground run, the first co-op governance that has to settle real disputes between real neighbours. Everything the city will need at full scale is invented here at the scale of a thousand, where a mistake is survivable.

## The trajectory: trucked in, then self-sufficient, then export-positive

**At the start everything is trucked in**, and the serial must never pretend otherwise. A settlement on open ground eats what arrives by lorry.

The whole arc of a village, in the game and in the ground, is to work itself off those deliveries one at a time: plant the food forest, grow what it eats, make its own power and materials, reach **self-sufficiency** — and then go past it, until it **exports more than it brings in**. Sovereignty is the end of that road, not the beginning of it: food, then energy, on land the Maia City Coop owns from the first day, which are the three of [self-sovereignty](../systems/self-sovereignty.md).

Saying "nothing is trucked in" is only true of the finished city in 2042. Of a new village it is a lie, and it throws away the most interesting thing the game has: the climb.

## Culture & Customs

The jump this track asks for is your old life. A founding settler does not visit; they move. They are drawn from the best players in [the twin](the-twin.md) and the guests of [the dome network](the-dome-network.md), plus founders and entrepreneurs who want to build the primitives themselves.

## Notable Features

- 500 to 1,500 people, which is small enough to govern face to face and large enough to need real systems.
- Mediterranean, so the climate suits year-round growing from the start.
- Every primitive the city depends on gets its first real test here.

## Current State

Not built at the open. It comes in order: the first hamlet, then the village site, then 500 settlers, then 1,500 with a measured self-sufficient year.
