# The Dome Network

Real domes, spread around the world, owned by the community together. Track 2, which opens second, when the first real dome leaves the game, and runs to 2042.

**One dome at a time, each the next size up, each delivered to one winner** chosen by a leaderboard inside [the twin](the-twin.md), and each opening a new produce category the city sells. The first is a 50 cm dome growing herbs and strawberries, on the same day the last dome size finishes standing in the game. From the 8 m size up, a dome has a bed and a kitchen, and that is the first night anyone can book.

## Description

One dome at a time, in real places, each one a working piece of the city rather than a show home: the geodesic greenhouse shell, the food growing in it, the systems that make it self-sufficient. A guest spends a night in the way of living the city is for, which is a stronger argument than any film of it.

Each dome grows. A single dome becomes a cluster, a cluster becomes a small village, and that is how track 2 turns into [the first village](the-first-village.md).

## Culture & Customs

The jump this track asks for is a trip. It is the second step: too large for an evening, far smaller than moving your life. The guests who come here are the pool the founding settlers are drawn from.

## Notable Features

- Bookable for a night, anywhere in the world.
- Each dome is owned through the co-ops, like everything else in the project.
- Each one is a real test of the dome systems before the city depends on them.
