# Maia City

A sovereign solarpunk city state, built from the ground up in 16 years by the people who live in it. When the story opens, it exists only in [the twin](the-twin.md), once for every European country, and the ground is empty. It is built for real in the country whose city in the game first reaches 1.3 million citizens.

The city is deliberately named for [Maia](../../characters/maia.md), and the name check flags that on purpose.

## Description

Geodesic domes, each one a greenhouse and a home at once, where people co-live and co-produce their food year-round. Permaculture food forests inside the domes and spread around them, so the city feeds itself without importing. Buildings grown as much as built: biomimicry and material science in the structure itself, a generation of cities that sit in harmony with the nature around them instead of paving it. Nothing moves on the surface. Logistics and transport run fully automated, 100% underground, so the ground level belongs to people, plants and weather.

## Culture & Customs

Nobody starves and nobody has to survive, so the whole of human attention goes to the problems worth cracking. There is no traditional work. There is ownership in what you believe in, through shared co-ops, and the whole of the city is owned by the people who live in it.

Thinking and executing big is the norm here, not the exception. That is the one cultural fact that separates this city from everywhere else, and the one the serial exists to prove is reachable.

## Notable Features

- Dome greenhouses for co-living and year-round food.
- Food forests, inside and around, for full self-sufficiency.
- [Self-sovereignty](../systems/self-sovereignty.md) three ways: its own food, its own energy, and all its land owned by the Maia City Coop.
- Dome factories where robots run around the clock.
- The underground: automated logistics and transport, with no surface traffic.
- Its own monetary policy and its own system of government.
- Maia as the city's AI mayor, carrying out what its owners intend.

## Current State

Not yet in the ground. Track 4 opens last, as the people arrive, with the land and the sovereignty; the city is what the first three tracks exist to make possible.

## Open

- The site. The region is not chosen yet, and the first village goes in the Mediterranean, so the city's own ground is a separate decision.
- Sovereignty. Whose land it stands on, and what the arrangement is, is undecided and is the hardest external question in the project.
