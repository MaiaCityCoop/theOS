# Self-Sovereignty

**The city's key pattern: it feeds itself, powers itself and owns itself.** Three sovereignties, at every scale the
city is built at: one dome, one village, one city. Together they are what makes nobody have to survive, which is
the whole of *living versus surviving* made physical.

## The three

| Sovereignty | What it means | Where it is built |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Food** | The city grows what it eats, year-round, in food forests inside the domes and around them, layered from the canopy down to the roots so that tending them is not backbreaking work. | [The domes](the-domes.md), and every produce category the domes open |
| **Energy** | The city makes the energy it uses, and is designed to need little of it: built for the sun from the first drawing, not given panels afterwards. Heat held in the mass of the building, machines that run on the sun while it shines, little to store. | [The domes](the-domes.md) |
| **Ownership** | Financial sovereignty. The Maia City Coop owns all the land, which means every citizen does, and Maia is owned by everyone too. Nobody is a tenant, nobody's ground can be pulled from under them, and the machine the city runs on is its citizens'. | [The stack](the-stack.md) |

Each one removes one of the things a person has to survive: hunger, the bill, the landlord. Remove all three and
what is left is the time to be your full size.

## Rules & Limitations

- **All three, or it is not sovereign.** A community with food and no ownership lives on somebody's land; one with
  ownership and no food lives on deliveries. The city is the place that has all three at once.
- **Sovereignty is the end of the road, not the beginning.** A new village starts with everything trucked in, and
  the serial never pretends otherwise. The story is the climb: food, then energy, then a self-sufficient year, then
  past it, until it exports more than it brings in. See [the first village](../locations/the-first-village.md).
- **It is measured, not declared.** A sovereignty counts the day it is measured standing (a harvest, a winter on its
  own energy, a deed in the coop's name), never the day it is announced.
- **It is never a shelter.** The city is self-sovereign because it is the better way to live, not to sit out
  something else. No episode sells it as protection from a collapse.
- **The person comes first.** Self-sovereignty is the citizen's before it is the city's: a person who does not have
  to survive is free to reach. The city state, with its own government, is what three sovereignties look like at the
  scale of a city.
- **How the money works comes later.** The ownership row says what is fixed. The coop's governance, its currency and
  its stake models come back with true citizenship.

## Practitioners

Every dome, from the 50 cm herb dome on somebody's windowsill (a first taste of food sovereignty) to the first village
and the city.

## Impact on Society

It is the city's proof and its export at once. Every place that copies the blueprint copies the three sovereignties,
and every one of them is a place where nobody has to survive.
