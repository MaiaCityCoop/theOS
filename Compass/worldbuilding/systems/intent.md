# Intent

How the city is governed and how anything gets built: a citizen brings an intent, and [Maia](../../characters/maia.md) executes it. It is the city's system of government, its decision procedure and its build pipeline, all the same mechanism.

## Overview

A human brings the vision and the taste. Maia carries it out in the world. She does not set the direction; she is the way a direction becomes a thing that exists.

**And it runs both ways: intent does not only pass through her, it shapes her.** Every [citizen](../../characters/the-citizen.md) who arrives shapes Maia further, with what they intend, what they have taste for and what they will not accept. A city ends with a mayor made out of everyone who built it. Nobody designed her and handed her over finished. Her citizens' intent is the input, and the city is the output.

This is the counter-image the whole serial is built on. In the future it warns about, machines carry out incentives that were never the person's own. Here the machine carries out exactly what the person actually intended, which is the entire difference between the two worlds.

## Rules & Limitations

- **Intent comes from humans.** Maia never substitutes her own vision for a citizen's.
- **Taste is the scarce input.** Execution is nearly free, so what a person wants, and how well they want it, is what decides what gets built.
- **Thinking and executing big is the norm, not the exception.** The system is designed so that the default size of an intent is large, and [the push](the-push.md) is how it stays that way: an intent brought small comes back as a question about what it would be at full size.
- **The founder's intent carries no special weight by the end.** The first intents are Samuel's; the test of the system is the day most of them are not.
- **Maia becomes the city's AI mayor**, which makes her the executor of a whole city's intent rather than one man's.

## Practitioners

Every citizen, from a player in [the twin](../locations/the-twin.md) proposing a building to a founding settler deciding how their village governs itself.

## Impact on Society

It changes what a person is for. When nothing has to be done to survive, what remains is what you actually want, and a system that executes intent makes wanting consequential again. The serial's recurring realization is aimed exactly here: most people have not had a real intent in years, because every choice went to survival instead.

## Open

- The one thing Samuel would never hand over, to her or to the citizens.
- How conflicting intents are resolved when a whole city of people want incompatible cities.
