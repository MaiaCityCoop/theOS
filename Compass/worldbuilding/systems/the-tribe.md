# The Tribe

The strategy sheet for how Maia City finds its people, brings them together and keeps them, in the game and for real. It is the reference for upgrading [the game](the-game.md)'s village ladder, so that every gate grows people as well as domes. Written with Samuel on 18 November 2026 (intent 0040), from what the best community builders have learned, in our own words. Sources are in the artifact library: `the-worst-way-to-build-a-tribe` ranks eleven ways to build a tribe.

## Overview

**A tribe grows from people, purpose and an engine, never from land first.** Every community that lasts has three things running from its first day:

- **a culture:** a clear way of life people choose;
- **an income:** it pays its own way;
- **a way in:** new people keep finding it, testing it and staying.

Land, buildings and infrastructure come after these three, and they come faster because of them.

Maia City runs on the three from day one. The vision is the signal. The €1-a-day citizenship and the village invitation are the filter. The village, from three settlers in tents to 1,597 in domes, is where people gather. **Signal, filter, gather:** that is our recruiting engine, and the game is its first container.

## Rules & Limitations

### The seven principles

1. **Signal before soil.** Say the big vision out loud, every day, in public, before there is anything to stand on. People come for the vision, then they build the place. *For us:* the race to build Europe's new capital, the daily story, built in public.
2. **Filter by commitment, not by a form.** The right people show themselves by what they put in: time, money, work. *For us:* citizenship costs €1 a day. Villages invite; they are never open to all.
3. **Gather in proximity, with a purpose.** Relationships form when people live and work side by side, and show who will go the distance. Living together without a shared project is only sharing a flat. *For us:* the shared domes, the village market, the coops.
4. **People and income first, land later.** The order decides everything. A place that has no income and no new people stays half-built, however beautiful the land. *For us:* the game comes first, then the real city; the starter pack is small; each gate's gap is its own investment round.
5. **Build shoulder to shoulder.** The deepest bonds come from making something together. A gathering is good; a build day is better. *For us:* founding a coop, raising a dome and every ten minutes a day are work done together.
6. **A clear way of life, or it is just housing.** A neighbourhood without an ethos ends up as another housing estate. Name what is sacred, how people treat each other, what a day looks like. *For us:* the Compass, and sparkling visions made the norm.
7. **Clear structures over endless consensus.** Flat consensus without structure breeds drama and stalls. Clear roles, clear rights and a vote that ends make people free to build. *For us:* the coops' governance, the hard-coded 50% payout, citizens holding at least 51%.

### What we take from it

- **Every gate grows people as well as domes.** A village climbs the ladder by the people it attracts, filters and keeps, not only by what it builds.
- **The income comes first.** A village earns from its first harvest (the village market) and, later, from hosting: visitors, guests and residencies.
- **Families need a low-risk way in.** Keep the job, keep the school, join for a season, then stay.
- **Proven tribes attract land.** Owners of empty land and resorts look for living communities. A village that runs its engine will be offered places to grow.

## History

Ten years of shared houses, eco-villages, raw-land projects and the first startup cities show the same lesson: the communities that grew solved money and people first. The ones that started with land, sacrifice or consensus alone rarely grew past their founders. Maia City starts where they learned to start.

## Practitioners

- **The signal:** Samuel and avenMAIA, every day, in public; later every village that tells its own story.
- **The filter:** every citizen's €1 a day; every village's invitation.
- **The gathering:** every village coop, which decides whom it invites and hosts.

## Impact on Society

If a tribe can be grown on purpose, belonging stops being luck. Anyone with a vision can find their people, live beside them and build with them. That is the norm we want, not the exception.

## Open: upgrades for the village ladder

Proposals from this sheet for [the game](the-game.md)'s ladder. None is decided until Samuel sets it in the game sheet.

1. **A guest step before villager.** A citizen can visit a village for a season (in the game, a few days; for real, one to three months) before being invited. Villages earn from hosting, and invitations come from people who have lived together. Possible meter: *guests hosted*, *guests invited*.
2. **A people gate beside the money gate.** Each Fibonacci gate needs its villagers *and* a recruiting engine running: for example, at least as many guests hosted as villagers to be added.
3. **Build days as play.** Raising a new dome is a shared event: everyone who helps on the day earns a share of its MINDS (the coop's stake). The game's version of the build party.
4. **The village's way of life.** At its founding, each village writes a short charter: what it is for and how it lives. It shows on the village page, so it can be chosen. It is the filter's other half.
5. **Hosting as an income line.** From the market dome at 5, a village can sell stays (a guest bed in a tent, later a living dome) for city HEARTS. This is the culture-first income, before any land is bought.
6. **The real pilot stays close to a city.** The pilot village is built near an existing city's roads, schools and jobs, so families can join without risking everything.
