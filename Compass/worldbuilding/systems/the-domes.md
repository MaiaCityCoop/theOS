# The Domes

The geodesic dome is the city's single building block: a home, a greenhouse and a farm at the same time. People co-live and co-produce their own food inside them, year-round.

## Overview

Each dome is a greenhouse shell enclosing a living space and a growing space that are not separated. Permaculture food forests run inside the domes and across the ground between them, so the city feeds itself rather than importing food. The structures themselves come out of biomimicry and material science: built the way living things are built, and sitting in harmony with the nature around them instead of replacing it.

Dome factories run on robots, around the clock, which is what makes building a whole city's housing possible inside 16 years.

## Rules & Limitations

- **Year-round food, and full self-sufficiency.** The food forests inside and around the domes are not decoration; the self-sufficiency claim depends on them. They are layered from the canopy down to the roots, so that tending them is not backbreaking work.
- **Designed for the sun from the first drawing.** A dome is not a house with panels added afterwards. Heat is held in its mass, its machines run on the sun while it shines, and it is built to need little energy, so there is little to store.
- **Co-living and co-production in the same space.** Living and growing are not zoned apart.
- **Regenerative construction.** Biomimetic structures and sustainable building practices, with the material science as part of the design rather than a finish.
- **Robots in the factories, 24/7.** Nobody works a shift to make this happen.
- **Every dome is tested in the real world before the city depends on it**, which is the job of [the dome network](../locations/the-dome-network.md) from year 2 and [the first village](../locations/the-first-village.md) from year 4.

## Impact on Society

The dome is why the city can be self-sufficient and sovereign at the same time. A city that imports its food or its energy is not sovereign, whatever its monetary policy says, so the greenhouse is a political fact as much as an agricultural one. It carries two of the city's three sovereignties, food and energy; the third, ownership, is [the stack](the-stack.md). See [self-sovereignty](self-sovereignty.md).
