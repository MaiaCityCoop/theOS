# The Underground

All logistics and transport, 100% underground and fully automated. Nothing moves on the surface.

## Overview

Goods, waste, materials and people travel below ground, carried by a network that runs itself. The purpose is not speed. It is that the surface of the whole city belongs entirely to people, plants and weather, with no roads, no parking, no deliveries and no traffic at ground level.

## Rules & Limitations

- **100% underground.** The surface carries no logistics at all; this is absolute, not a majority.
- **Fully automated.** No drivers, no shifts, no one working the network to keep it alive.
- **It is what makes the food forests possible.** Without surface transport, the ground between the domes can be growing instead of paving.
- **It has to exist before the city scales.** [The first village](../locations/the-first-village.md) builds the first real run of it, at a scale where getting it wrong is survivable.

## Impact on Society

A city with no surface traffic is a different experience of being outdoors, and it is one of the few claims in the project that a visitor feels immediately rather than having to be told. For the serial that makes it a strong thing to show: the silence is the argument.

## Open
