# One camera

## What happened

- [0003] There were two cameras that did not know each other. Now there is one, and its height decides everything.
- [0003] Scroll from inside the tent up to the skyline, twelve kilometres above, in one move.
- [0003] The same on a phone: a joystick, two fingers to rise.
- [0003] Walls, rocks and trunks stop you; a tent is entered by its door.
