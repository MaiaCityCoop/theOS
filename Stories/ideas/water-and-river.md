# Water and river

## What happened

- [0004] The river was a ditch of one even width. Now it widens in its bends, cuts a steep bank on the outside and lays a bar on the inside.
- [0008] Its gravel bar was made after a real one: the Isar's.
- [0004] The water: a notch more paradise blue, no more.
- [0005] Still to come: reeds in the shallows, driftwood caught on the bars.
- [0006] Still to come: a current you can read, fast along the cut bank, slow in a bar's lee.
