# Forest floor

## What happened

- [0008] Each kind of tree lays its own litter: needles under the pines, leaves under the oaks.
- [0011] "A very flat looking, bad biome floor": it was a print. It was painted again with depth: needles thick and thin, twigs, cones, moss, the hollows dark.
- [0011] Things now lie on it: fallen branches, cushions of moss.
- [0011] The first tries were wrong: cones like loaves of bread, twigs like noodles.

## Samuel's own words

- [0011] "shouldn't the ground floor itself directly be way more realistic, three-dimensional, with also sticks, moss etc.? currently it is a very flat looking, bad biome floor."

## What the world can show

- Low over the needles
- A fallen branch in evening light
- Moss cushions in the shade
