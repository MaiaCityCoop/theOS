# Trees and forest

## What happened

- [0008] The wish: a real tree, with branches and needles, not blocks. Ten times the realism. One pine first, made perfect.
- [0008] Held against photos of a stone pine, the first try failed: a short thick trunk and a flat plate. The real one has a slender trunk bending in an S, six limbs like an umbrella's ribs, a dome of rounded puffs.
- [0008] Every tree was alike. Now a stand has its age: saplings in the glades among trees of a hundred and fifty years.
- [0008] A second kind joined: the holm oak, dark and round among the pines.
- [0008] The forest carries on in groves and glades to the horizon, and nothing pops in or out as you move.
- [0010] The leaves flickered between light and shade, even without wind. Five stills a second apart found why; now they hold still.

## Samuel's own words

- [0010] "the tree leaves have weird flickering, even without wind. maybe it can be the fast moving sun?"
