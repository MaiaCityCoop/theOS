# Clouds

## What happened

- [0008] One layer of cumulus that drifts with the wind and lays its shade on everything below.
- [0008] The first clouds repeated like wallpaper; the repeat had to be broken until no two looked alike.
- [0008] Fly above their layer and the land disappears under them.
- [0009] The city's plots were drawn on top of the clouds: wrong, and mended, so a cloud hides a village as it hides a meadow.
- [0010] They were too slow: now a cloud crosses the sky in a few minutes.
