# Rocks and ground

## What happened

- [0004] A rule was set: every small thing, even a stone, starts in the library before it is scattered in the world.
- [0008] The ground's textures were made anew: curled plates of dried mud, bedded rock, sand rippled by the wind.
- [0008] Boulders became bodies: you walk round them, not through them.
