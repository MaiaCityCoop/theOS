This is the story of how one million humans build their own solarpunk city, from the ground up. In just 16 years.

Today the city is one stop long. The stop is a meadow. A river runs along one side and a tent stands in the middle.

Cities do not start with a skyline. They start with a place you can point at and a sign that says where you are. This is the sign.
