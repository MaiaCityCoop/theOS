The Maia City AlmanacThe method, in fullp. 02

Three steps. Two are done.

We have never built a city. That is why this guide is short, and why it is written one day at a time, 5423 of them.

It goes first in game, then in real. You build it where mistakes cost nothing, then where they cost bricks.

“It all starts with a green meadow and one tent.”

Samuel, day 1

(the one human so far)

    Find a meadow.

    Maia looked for one. There was none. So she made one: land that never ends, and one river. No trees. No sun. No shadows. Not yet.

    Done · day 1
  2. 2

    Put up one tent.

    One tent. Nobody in it yet. For now that is the whole skyline, and you can see all of it from anywhere.

    Add 999,999 more of you.
