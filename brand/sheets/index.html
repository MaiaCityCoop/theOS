Eight brands for Maia City. Each one a whole page, in its own voice.

Not sheets this time: eight landing pages, each with its own idea, layout, type, copy and things to touch. They show the app, the game and pieces that might come later, never the work board. Open one, scroll it, play with it. Then say which one, or which parts.
